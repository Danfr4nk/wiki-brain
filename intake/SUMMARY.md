## What this cannot tell you

It records what was **logged**, not what happened. An unlogged night is indistinguishable here from a night with nothing in it, and no figure above corrects for that. Where a unit closed with quantity unaccounted for, the reconciliation names the gap rather than distributing it across the doses that were recorded — so a total can be lower than what was actually consumed, and the report says which.
