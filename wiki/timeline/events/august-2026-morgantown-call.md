# The Morgantown St Call and the Leverage Campaign, August 16–19 2026

Between 23:37 on Saturday 16 August 2026 and 15:15 on Wednesday 19 August,
Dan and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] exchanged **1,199 messages** — 811
from Dan, 388 inbound — and the ten-year relationship ended. The window
opens with a phone call Dan did not place and recorded anyway, and closes
with an argument about something neither party ever names, and a declared
block that the record shows was not executed.

The thing that makes this window different from the eight or nine severances
before it is not its intensity. It is that **for fifteen minutes and
twenty-seven seconds there is a primary artifact of the third party's
conduct that is not anybody's testimony about it.** Every prior account of
[[wiki/people/jerel-coles|Coles]] in this wiki is mediated — Annie's claims,
Dan's inferences, a group chat, a background check. On the night of August 16
he is audible.

## LLM Quick Brief

**For context injection:** 16–19 August 2026, Uniontown. Aug 16 evening: Annie
flakes on a $60 procurement handoff; Dan spends five hours escalating, then at
~23:37 answers a call from her number and finds Coles on the line. He records
**15:27** of it on his MacBook (`Morgantown St.m4a`, finalised 23:54:49). On
tape: Coles admits going through her phone while she slept, refuses to return
it, screams at her to say Dan pimped her out, threatens her parents, and tells
Dan to kill himself; Dan's near-sole contribution is *"give her her phone and
let her leave,"* said twenty times, and he offers to drive over. Messages
sent from Annie's handle during the call — *"She's a slut,"* *"You made me
fuck guys for money"* — are **Coles typing on her phone, not Annie**. Aug
17–19: Dan runs a three-day campaign to send the audio to
[[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen]]'s work email; Annie discloses a suicide
attempt by strangulation; Dan falsely claims to have sent it on Aug 18 as a
deliberate probe (*"it wasn't actually sent, and I knew you would suddenly
come back to life"*); Coles seizes her phone a second time on Aug 18 at 21:46;
Annie sends **SOS** six times between 22:18 and 23:41 and Dan replies 72
minutes later. Aug 19 is a eulogy in the morning and, in the afternoon, an
hour of argument about a future act **neither party ever states**; Dan says
*"Goodbye. I am blocking"* at 15:07 and then sends seven more messages, and the
export ends mid-exchange at 15:15:33. At 15:12 he says the archive is retained
and unused (*"I could have torn your life apart. I still could and I don't"*),
contradicting his own claims of having sent it. Whether the email was ever
actually sent is **unresolved**. The one structurally new act is at 14:53:25:
he closes the Milo channel in advance — the vector every prior reconciliation
ran through.

## When the recording actually ran

The container says the file was written at **23:54:49 EDT**. That is not the
same as when the call happened, and the difference matters because Dan's
outbound texts overlap the call and are the only clock in the room.

Two independent alignments fix the start:

- Audio **07:51** — Coles: *"I've never fought anybody in my life."* Text
  **23:44:21** — Dan: *"if you think im going to fight you then you literally
  know nothing about me."* → t₀ ≈ 23:36:30.
- Audio **13:38** — Dan: *"Or I'm going to send this whole thing to Ellen."*
  Text **23:51:45** — Dan: *"Calling Ellen with the video of what just
  happened."* → t₀ ≈ 23:38:07.

So the call ran roughly **23:37 → 23:53**, and the memo was saved about two
minutes after it ended. The recording opens mid-shout — Coles already
screaming *"Call her, Dan!"* over Annie begging — which means it does not
capture the call's own beginning. Dan's *"You call and hang up that's nice"*
at **23:39:58** sits inside the recording rather than before it, so there was
at least one earlier connection attempt.

**The filename is location evidence.** macOS Voice Memos names a recording
after the street the recording device is standing on. `Morgantown St` is
**Dan's** street — [[wiki/legal/463-morgantown|463 Morgantown St]], the house
he shares with [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]. Coles's address of record is
106 Nassau St, Uniontown. The file is therefore weak but real corroboration
that Dan was at home, on a call, and not at the scene — which is what the
recording's content independently shows and what the corrections section below
had to establish against a source that said the opposite.

## The night — August 16

The five hours before the call are the ordinary failure mode of this
relationship, not a prelude to anything. Annie asked at 16:38 to see Dan
(*"I will see you when I get home… So try to see bop"*), Dan read it as a
procurement request dressed as a visit, and she then went silent for six
hours while he escalated alone: 64 messages that day against 12 inbound. At
**22:27** he gives up on the handoff — *"You'll need to email me if or when
you want to arrange to get your $60"* — and at 23:16, still trying, *"Do you
want this money or not????"*

At **23:25:23** he writes the sentence the next three days turn on: *"I
actually would like to find out if you told me not to respond the other day
to con me so you didn't get caught."* That refers to **August 13**, when
Annie had messaged *"He sent a message to a group chat / With you / I'm just
asking you to please. Do not engage,"* and Dan had agreed — *"I say
nothing"* — on the stated understanding that she had cut Coles off.

### What is on the recording

**Verified against the primary transcript, 2026-08-20.** What follows is no
longer attributed; it is checked. Where a claim moved, the old version is kept
visible.

**The shape of the call, before any quotation.** 204 speaker turns in fifteen
minutes: **Coles 99, Annie 55, Dan 50.** Coles takes nearly as many turns as
the other two combined, and the call is his throughout — he sets every topic,
and both of the others are responding to him.

**What holds, and is now primary.**

- **Coles confesses to searching her phone while she slept**, at **00:43**:
  *"She was sleeping, I went through her phone. Yeah, and now what?"*

  ***"Yeah, I am"*** — twice, at 09:19 and 09:31. Annie: *"You're holding my
