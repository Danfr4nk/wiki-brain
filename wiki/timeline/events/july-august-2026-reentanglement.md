# The Re-entanglement, July 26 – August 2 2026

In the eight days after the goodbye that closed
[[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact|the July 2026 re-contact]], Dan
and [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] exchanged **1,880 messages** and met
in person **six times**. Five of those meetings were drug handoffs. The
sixth was the first, and the exchange did not survive it in any recognisable
form: what began the window as an accusation of attempted assault ended it,
on August 2, with Annie walking to Dan's house to apologise to his mother.

What this window establishes is not that the relationship resumed — the
re-contact already established that. It is that **the relationship
reorganised around procurement as its only physical form**, and that both
parties know it and say so. Dan states the terms three separate times
("stop contacting me if you want me as your friend or dealer or anything
other than wanting to be together") and then supplies anyway, every time he
is asked, within hours. The severance failed in July; in this window it
stops being a severance at all and becomes a working arrangement with a
schedule.

## LLM Quick Brief

**For context injection:** July 26 – August 2, 2026. 1,880 messages, 969
from Dan, 911 from Annie. Opens at 05:22 on July 26 with Dan concluding
that the previous night's invitation to Hopwood was an ambush, publishing
the full text log at `caakehorn.github.io/leviathan/transcript.html`, and
messaging Annie's mother [[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen]] at 06:22 in the
belief that Annie was in physical danger. Annie's account: the man she says
raped her on July 25 came to her uncle's bedroom window while she slept,
poured water on her, threw her belongings, filmed himself asking "did I
rape you," took her phone, and used it to message Dan. Four suicidal
statements from Annie in three days. The transcript comes down at 18:05 at
her request and is replaced by an access-gated decoy site. Procurement
restarts July 27 and runs five handoffs in six days ($40, $100, ~$50, one
undisclosed, $50). A twelve-hour standoff on July 28 over whether Dan will
email her parents ends with the email never sent. The window closes on
August 2 with Annie apologising to [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]] in
person, then buying $50 from Dan forty minutes later. Relationship status:
**not severed, not resumed — supplied.**

## The seventeen hours of July 26

The single most compressed sequence in the corpus. It is worth taking
slowly because the shape of the whole window is in it.

**05:22 — the ambush conclusion.** Dan wakes to a realisation about the
previous night: "i just now realized. this was not sent to me by accident by
you. you were with him…again." Within fourteen minutes he has published the
message log and written "I'm not afraid of the truth."

**05:39–05:57 — the accusation.** The replies that come back are not
Annie's. They are the man's, from her phone: "The cuck never gives up," "Had
her fuck old men for drugs," and then the charge that organises the next
week — "I have video proof how you fingered her for hours while she was
unconscious." Dan's response is forensic rather than defensive, and it is a
good example of what [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|the method]] does
under fire: he dates the video against timestamped footage from before and
after, notes that Annie's hair is wet in it, and observes that Betty — Annie's
chihuahua, the aggressive one of the pair Dan and Annie raised together
(see [[wiki/people/milo]]) — would not have stayed silent, and was in fact
shut out of the room as she always was during sex.

**06:22 — the disclosure, executed.** "Alright I'm contacting Ellen." Dan
sends the screenshots to Annie's mother. This matters out of all proportion
to its length: the wiki has recorded **six or more** "I'm telling your mom"
threats from Dan across the relationship and **none previously carried out**.
Sixteen minutes elapse between the decision and the act.

**06:42–07:22 — the reversal of blame.** Annie's first response is not to
the rape allegation but to the disclosure: "Please Dan / Do not." Then the
correction that Dan reads as the collapse of the whole story: the police
were involved because the man took her phone, "NOT BC HE ASSAULTED ME." She
is angry at Dan and not at the man, and Dan says so eleven times in forty
minutes. Her position: "god yeah I'm gonna get sent the fuck away / Bye /
Kill ne / Fucking just kill me."

**08:10–09:51 — the body comment.** Dan sees a photograph and attacks
Annie's weight gain: "bad time to get off stimulants i guess," "hey you'll
get to buy lots of new clothes." He defends it by citing six months of her
jokes about his own weight ("size 36 pants because i was eating domino's
during covid"). **This is the only injury Annie returns to unprompted
across the entire window** — twice on July 26 and never conceded. Dan
withdraws it completely twenty-eight hours later: "You were perfect when you
looked like an anemic 80 pounds and you just as perfect now that you're
healthy."

**12:08–13:11 — the ultimatum.** Dan sets the condition that governs the
rest of the week: "please do not contact me unless you have decided to
completely and fully disassociate yourself with this person." Annie: "We are
no longer speaking. Believe me."

**17:20 — the concession.** "No I'm not okay. I need to seek help. I am
ruining myself and everyone else in my life."

**18:00–18:05 — the retraction.** Annie: "why would you send him our text
log Dan… can you please find some way to make it private. That is not
alright." Dan had sent it to *her*; the man read it from her phone. He takes
the site down without argument — "The logs will be offline in 30 seconds"
— and confirms at 18:05, "It's down." The forensic apparatus, built over
months and deployed as leverage three days earlier, is withdrawn on one
request in under five minutes.

**19:06 — the scratch-off.** Dan buys a crossword scratch ticket, does not
scratch it, and locks it away: "I will hold on to this scratch off in case
eggie bagels ever finds her way back." It becomes the window's running
wager (see below).

**20:20 — the reset.** "Omg he caught one." Milo has caught a rabbit.
Fifteen hours after "Don't contact me again," and with nothing resolved, no
apology given and no position withdrawn, the register returns to the dog.
Annie: "Bunny? / Omg / He caught it?"

Two features of this table are the point. First, **the frequency**: five
handoffs in six days, against a fifty-two-day severance immediately prior
and against Dan's own refusal on July 26 — which held for **thirty-one
hours**. Second, **the disclosure inside it**: on July 27 Dan asks the
obvious question — "annie I'm so scared and what if you. Get drug tested
again" — receives "My parents know. That it won't be out of my system. They
know. I have been honest," and delivers fifteen minutes later. The stated
recovery programme and the supply relationship are running in the same
thread on the same afternoon, and neither party treats this as a
contradiction.

Dan names the arrangement's real function himself, unprompted, at 23:04 on
July 28, in the middle of an argument about something else: **"even the
worst thing I've done — getting you drugs — didn't really benefit me nearly
as much as it was about that being the only way you would see me."** This is
the thesis of [[wiki/mind/synthesis/dan-annie-fallout-verdict|the fallout
verdict]] and of [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|the supply network]]
stated in the operator's own voice, against his own interest, while the
behaviour it describes is still running.

## July 28: the disclosure standoff, and the rule it produces

Seven hundred and twenty-one messages between 11:48 and 23:59 — the largest
single-day volume in the window and the most sustained argument in it. Dan
announces at 11:48 that he will email Annie's parents; the stated purpose is
not her safety but his reputation: "all I want is to clear MY name."

Annie's resistance escalates through every register she has. Petition
("I am begging from the bottom of my heart"). Bargaining ("I will never ask
anything of you ever again"). Jurisdiction ("It HAPPENED TO ME. NOT YOU.
ITS NOT YOUR DECISION… It's my fucking body"). Self-harm ("I'll fucking
hurt myself. Idgaf. Nothing is worth this madness anymore. I give up").
Surrender ("Do whatever you want. My life is already ruined"). Dan holds
the position for twelve hours across roughly two hundred messages, tells
her he will show her the text before sending, and at 23:54 floats delegating
it: "I'm literally considering having rick give it to them so it's not
coming from ME."

**The email was never sent.** Nothing in the record after this night
references it, and by the afternoon of July 30 the thread is about a
petting zoo at the library.

That bears on the pattern
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/block-unblock-loop|the block/unblock loop]] records —
the eighteen block threats and six-plus maternal-disclosure threats that are
never executed. Two contacts with Annie's mother appear in this window,
forty-eight hours apart:

- The July 26 disclosure was **impulsive**, made under a genuine belief that
  Annie was in danger, and made in sixteen minutes.
- The July 28 disclosure was **deliberated**, announced twelve hours in
  advance, negotiated over, drafted, offered for pre-approval, and never
  sent.

## What Annie is doing, on her own account

Recorded as her claims, not as findings. Several are unverifiable from
inside the corpus and one is contradicted by conduct in the same thread.

She has come clean to her parents about "EVERYTHING," including telling them
herself that it was the man and not Dan who had been harassing Dan and Suz.
She has had a first counselling appointment and has agreed to therapy three
times a week **as an alternative to being sent away** for months without
outside contact. She has taken a drug test administered by her father. She
is grounded, is not permitted to drive, and is not left unsupervised. She is
walking for her mental health and describes the resulting state as a "high
from being active." On July 28 she reports, in capitals, going to the
library and getting a book. She is working shifts, moving between her
parents' house and her uncle's in Hopwood, and reports crying herself to
sleep nightly.

She also asks Dan for drugs on five of the next six days.

The corpus should not resolve this into hypocrisy. The more useful reading
is the one the record actually supports: her recovery programme and her
procurement channel are addressed to different people and do not know about
each other, except that Dan is told about both and supplies anyway.

## The man with no name

The third party is now the most consequential undocumented person in the
corpus, and Dan has made a deliberate decision not to identify him. He
states it twice: "I don't even want to know their fucking name and have made
a point to not even find out," and, more revealingly, "the reason I won't
even learn who this is, is because I do not want to live my life with the
HATRED that I would feel towards one person."

This is a documented act of **chosen ignorance by a person whose defining
method is exhaustive documentation**, and it is the sharpest counter-example
the corpus holds to [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|the forensic
method]]'s claim to generality. Everything else in Dan's life is
instrumented. This one node is left dark on purpose, and the stated reason
is emotional load-bearing rather than epistemic. Full page:
[[wiki/people/the-unnamed-man]].

## The apparatus, in four states

Six days is enough to see the whole range of what
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|the Leviathan build]] is for.

1. **Exhibit** (Jul 26, 05:36). `transcript.html` published and sent, framed
   as "I'm not afraid of the truth."
2. **Retracted** (Jul 26, 18:00–18:05). Taken offline on one request from
   its subject, in under five minutes, without argument.
3. **Fortified** (Jul 26 22:41 – Jul 31). Rebuilt behind a five-second
   secret-pattern gate that routes failures into a decoy: a loading bar that
   *decelerates* as it approaches completion, ending at another fake page,
   looping indefinitely, with roughly forty pop-ups per layer. By July 31 the
   real site requires two passwords and a legally-binding disclaimer that
   logs device information and includes a no-trespass covenant
   (`caakehorn.github.io/leviathan/void.html`). Dan's stated motive is not
   security: "sometimes i need a win to feel better too. this just feels like
   outsmarting someone with the intelligence of an actual child."
4. **Gift** (Jul 31, 21:53). Dan shows Annie the wiki itself — the knowledge
   graph, with her as "the big pink dot" — and sends her the page describing
   how they met. "I've spent a lot of time writing out as much of my life
   into story form as I possibly could… it organized it into basically my own
   personal wikipedia."

The fourth state is the one that matters to this repository, because it is
**the wiki appearing inside its own primary source**, shown to its most
documented subject, who replies "That is pretty interesting." It also
produced a correction delivered by text message: "i need to fix the thing at
the top, it should say that I left my relationship within 24 hours…not you"
— a factual amendment to [[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015|the 2015
bond switch]] arriving over iMessage from the person the page is about.

## The scratch-off

On July 26 Dan buys a crossword scratch-off for Annie and refuses to scratch
it, holding it as a token contingent on seeing her again — "in case eggie
bagels ever finds her way back." When the driveway handoff happens the next
day he rules it insufficient: "your scratch off stays locked up and
unchecked unless I ACTUALLY get to see you. This doesn't count." On August 1,
after five days of contact and four meetings, he retires it: "I think I lose
already and it's time to get rid of your scratch off."

The object is worth recording because of what it discloses about the
accounting. Four in-person meetings did not satisfy a condition that a
single unpurchased visit would have. The wager was never on contact; it was
on a visit with no transaction attached — and the window contains exactly
one of those, on August 2, one day after he declared the bet lost.

## August 2: the apology

The window's last day is the only event in it that neither party's prior
behaviour predicted, and Dan had predicted it in the abstract the day
before. On August 1 Annie says Suz will never forgive her and that this is
right; Dan replies that Suz would forgive her in thirty seconds and that
Annie "just want[s] the easy way out all the time." Annie: "You are right."

Twenty-seven hours later she walks to the house — declining the arranged
meeting point, leaving Dan waiting on a street corner for half an hour
wondering if he has been set up again — and apologises to Suzanne in person.
Her account: "I have been wanting to speak especially with your mom… It
weighs on me every single day… neither of you, and especially her; someone
who had nothing to do with our relationship, was harassed for no reason."
She reports that they talked about abusive situations and about suppressed
things people avoid working through.

Dan's response is the only unqualified gratitude in eight days: "you don't
know how much psychic trauma you just spared us all," followed immediately
by "I did not think you would have the courage or the will to do that so I
was wrong."

Forty minutes later she asks, "Do you have it?" He drives to Sugie's with
fifty dollars' worth, crushed, and a straw.

That sequence — the hardest repair either of them has made, followed inside
the hour by the transaction that is now the relationship's only reliable
form — is the window in miniature, and it is why this page does not describe
the eight days as a reconciliation.

## What this settles

**It settles that June 1 was not a severance and July 26 was not an
ending.** Two goodbyes are now on record, fifty-two days apart, followed by
the highest-density contact period since March 2026.

**It settles what the relationship is currently made of.** Not cohabitation,
not a shared future, not — on the evidence of five days of Dan asking — a
stated mutual want. It is a supply relationship with an affectionate
register and a dog attached, and Dan describes it in exactly those terms
before continuing to run it.

**It gives the no-exit mechanism a stated cause.** On July 28 Dan explains
why he cannot close the loop: "You won't just fucking say you don't want to
be with me anymore. You've never once said it… And I'm fucking autistic. My
brain can't cross out that part of my life that I love unless I know that
you don't feel that way." Across 1,880 messages under repeated direct
demand, **Annie never makes the statement.** Her nearest approach is its
opposite — "Always have. Always fucking will," at 00:02 on July 29. This is
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/dormancy-not-exit|dormancy-not-exit]] with the
mechanism supplied by the subject: the tie survives because the terminating
sentence is withheld, and the withholding is what the loop runs on.

## What came next, and what it does to this page's reading

This window closes on August 2 with the relationship described here as
**"not severed, not resumed — supplied."** That held for seventeen more days
and then failed, and the failure is instructive about which half of the
formula was load-bearing.

Between August 10 and 16 the arrangement briefly stopped being only supply:
1,136 messages at a near-even ratio (652/484, against 3:1 in this window), a
sleepover on the night of the 10th, and Dan writing *"being next to you this
morning all i could think is how glad i was that i chose to go through all of
this."* The supply never stopped, but for one week it was not the only
structure.

Then on **August 13** Coles messaged a group chat containing Dan, and Annie
asked Dan not to reply — *"I'm just asking you to please. Do not engage."* He
agreed. Three nights later Coles called Dan from Annie's phone, and Dan read
that request retroactively as a concealment rather than a de-escalation. The
severance that follows is documented at
[[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call]] and ends the relationship
on August 19.

**The reading this page should carry forward:** supply was not what held the
relationship together, it was what let it keep meeting. When it failed in the
most ordinary way available — a $60 handoff she did not show up for, on the
evening of August 16 — five hours of escalation followed and the relationship
did not survive the week. A structure that can be ended by a missed
appointment was never the structure.

One item on this page is also now upgraded rather than corrected: the July 26
messages sent to Dan *from Annie's phone by Coles* were the corpus's first
instance of that. There have since been two more, on August 16 and August 18.
It is a **pattern**, not an incident, and it means any message attributed to
Annie's handle during a Coles-present episode needs the episode checked first.
