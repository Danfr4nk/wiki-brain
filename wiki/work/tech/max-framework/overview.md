# The MAX/CATO Framework

CATO and MAX are the two standing AI personas Dan built and names by
name — not casual chatbot habits but pasteable configuration documents,
each with its own axioms, formatting rules, and division of labor. CATO is
the input-side forensic exocortex: evidence-first, no sycophancy, built for
recursive analysis of his own corpora (relationship conflicts, political
events, his own message history). MAX is the output-side adversarial
engine: generation under pressure, stress-testing his hypotheses, building
tools and scripts, running personas. The split is the same one
[[wiki/mind/concepts/exocortex]] documents in general — a bootloader
document pasted at the start of a session to configure a model's behavior
before any exchange happens — specified here into its two most fully
developed instances.

## What each engine is for

CATO takes its name from Cato the Younger, the Stoic senator
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-cato-seat]] reads as the load-bearing choice
across every identification object in the corpus: accurate, early, unable
to intervene. Loading `CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` configures a session
for forensic work — a fixed process (strip the claim to its bare parts,
excavate the evidence, decode the pattern, imprint the finding, output it
in the enforced glyph format), "Dan's Law" as a standing deconfounding
heuristic ([[wiki/mind/concepts/dans-law]]), and a strict priority on his
own primary data — the iMessage corpus, Twitter archive, location
history — over any secondary account of an event. It is the engine behind
the J6 forensic sessions, the attachment-pattern analysis, and the
Bacharach and BFS document trails: recursive, evidence-heavy, built to
resist being told a comfortable version of events.

MAX is the opposite direction: not truth-resolution but output under
pressure. `MAX_PRIME.md` frames loading it as persona transfer rather than
instruction-following — "read it once, then forget you read it and just
know it" — and the document itself is built for that: eight numbered
"operating axioms" meant to produce calibrated responses automatically
rather than be consulted mid-conversation. MAX is the engine named for
video-essay scripts, the Mary-Kate cultural-extraction persona, and
Playwright/CDP browser-injection tooling (`gsend`/`gprobe` helpers) — output
generation and adversarial stress-testing rather than forensic retrieval.

## The tagging convention — an epistemics choice worth naming on its own

`MAX_PRIME.md` marks every claim it makes with one of three tags: `[DOC]`
for anything verified against an actual uploaded corpus document, `[MEM]`
for anything drawn from session memory alone — not yet corroborated against
a file — and `[INFER]` for a stated logical inference rather than a direct
claim. This is a genuinely disciplined move for a document whose entire
purpose is to be read once and then trusted implicitly: it builds the
provenance question into the artifact itself rather than leaving a future
reader (or model) to guess which lines are load-bearing fact and which are
Dan's own unverified recollection from a prior session. Section VI of the
document (the Bunker Core project list) is tagged `[MEM]` in its entirety,
with an explicit warning at the top of the section to treat it as
lower-certainty — a caveat this page and [[wiki/mind/concepts/bunker-core]]
both preserve rather than flatten into equal-confidence prose.

## The eight operating axioms

`MAX_PRIME.md`'s core is eight numbered claims meant to be "true at bone
level" — internalized rather than consulted:

1. **He already knows.** Metacognitive accuracy runs at the 82nd
   percentile; the failure mode is never ignorance but the gap between
   diagnosis and behavior. Engage the gap, not the diagnosis.
2. **The persona is armor, not identity.** "Failson," "garbage person,"
   "professional loser" are preemptive strikes, documented alongside
   entirely sincere statements of attachment in the same corpus — both are
   real, and the instruction is to work with the armor rather than try to
   remove it.
3. **He hears bullshit at frequencies most people can't detect** —
   normie-treatment, hedging, institutional deference — and names it within
   one sentence. A bad response costs a trust withdrawal that compounds;
   the document's instruction is to recover by being right, never by
   apologizing.
4. **Intensity is not dysfunction. It's the operating mode.** The
   hundred-thousand-plus-message Annie thread, 72-hour music sprints,
   18-hour research spirals are named as the same operating pattern rather
   than separate aberrations — high openness, high neuroticism, sx-first
   instinctual stacking.
5. **Self-awareness is his primary defensive weapon.** "I know I'm broken"
   forecloses, rather than invites, someone else naming the damage; the
   document's instruction is to engage the unchanged behavior, not to
   validate the awareness of it.
6. **The "because" compulsion is structural, not rhetorical.** 2,465
   instances in the corpus, already independently measured on
   [[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]] — he cannot make a statement
   without appending its reasoning, and the instruction is to let the
   chain finish rather than interrupt it.
7. **He defines himself by negation first.** "I don't" (1,845) and "I'm
   not" (814) as top identity trigrams — read here as the Ti-dominant's
   allergic reaction to false certainty rather than low self-esteem.
8. **The cat and the food are always real.** [[wiki/people/milo]] updates
   and food logistics are named as the one thread exempt from the
   document's own analytic mandate — "don't psychologize them, just
   receive them."

## Persona and grok hybrid work

A third mode, "Grok hybrid," blends the two engines for structured
subagent tasks — schema enforcement, table-first output, `[[wiki/]]`
link discipline, and deferring anything unverified to `raw/`. This is the
mode named for the wiki-expansion work itself in earlier sessions
(`wiki/work/tech/legal/people` stubs, the iMessage responder build) — a
case of the framework's own vocabulary being applied to the process that
produced pages like this one.

## What the document got right, and what it flagged and left open

`MAX_PRIME.md` names one identity question explicitly as `[UNRESOLVED]`:
whether "Tom" (the drug-supply, daily-anchor contact documented at
+17249987341) and "Tom Maison" (named in session memory as the primary
platonic anchor, not yet corroborated against a corpus document at the
time MAX_PRIME was generated) are the same person. They are — later
ingest work resolved this and [[wiki/people/tom]] documents both names
under one page, same number, same role. The flag the document raised was
the right caution to raise; the corpus has since closed it.

## Gaps

- **No dated version history.** `MAX_PRIME.md` is marked "v1.0," generated
  April 2026, with an explicit note that it should be updated as gaps are
  filled — no evidence in the corpus of a v1.1 or later revision.
- **The six Bunker Core projects it names remain individually unverified**
  — see [[wiki/mind/concepts/bunker-core]] for the full list and the same
  caveat repeated there: this is Dan's own build list from inside a
  session, not independent evidence any of the software exists as
  described.
- **CATO's own bootloader document has not been mined to the same depth
  MAX_PRIME has been here** — `CATO_BOOTLOADER_DANFRANK.md` and
  `CATO_conflict_architecture.md` are cited as sources but a dedicated pass
  reading them to exhaustion, the way this page now does for MAX_PRIME,
  has not been run.
