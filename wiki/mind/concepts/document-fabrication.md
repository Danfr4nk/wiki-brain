# Document Fabrication

On **14 August 2026**, between a counselling appointment and a drug order,
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] asked Dan to manufacture a passing drug
screen from Fayette County Drug and Alcohol to show her parents. He said yes
without asking a question, and spent the next forty minutes iterating on it.

This page exists because the wiki did not know he could do this, and because
everything else the wiki says about how Dan handles evidence has to be read
next to it.

## The exchange

> **13:08** — Annie: *"Do me favor lol make me an AI drug test results for
> fayette county drug and alcohol that shows passing 😂"*
> **13:08** — *"My parents asked me to show results"*
> **13:08** — *"Okay bye gotta go"* — she is walking into counselling
>
> **13:17** — Dan: *[image]* *"Something like this"*
>
> **14:05** — Annie: *"Send again."*
> **14:08** — Dan: *[image]* *"If so there's still stuff that needs fixed"*
> **14:09** — *"I got it pretty far but I didn't know if I needed to start
> from scratch or not"*
> **14:09** — Annie: *"Look the logo / Up"*
> **14:10** — Dan: *"The logo / 'Panal' instead of panel / The signature"*
> **14:10** — *"Find more that needs fixed I have to make the changes one at
> a time"*
> **14:11** — Annie: *[image]*
> **14:13** — Dan: *"Next time you need to give me more time lol / I can do
> these real good but I need time to tweak things"*
> **14:17** — *"That logo is really wide for a header / Should I center it?"*
> **14:18** — ***"Are you SENDING this to them or just going to SHOW it on
> your phone / If you're sending it we need to do a lot more work here"***

Six minutes later he is asking whether she wants him to take out $60 for the
next handoff.

## What each line of that is evidence of

**`"I can do these real good"`** — the plural, and the claim of proficiency.
This is the only documented instance in the corpus, but Dan does not describe
it as a first attempt. Whether there are others is an open question this page
cannot close.

**The error census.** Dan's own list of what is wrong with draft one is
`the logo / "Panal" instead of panel / the signature`. He is auditing his own
forgery the way he audits everything else — against the artifact's internal
consistency, not against whether it looks nice. The misspelling of *panel* is
the kind of defect that would fail an actual inspection, and he catches it
before the customer does.

**`"Are you SENDING this to them or just going to SHOW it on your phone"`** is
the load-bearing sentence. It is a **threat-model question**: a document shown
on a phone screen for four seconds needs to survive a glance; a document
emailed to a parent can be zoomed, forwarded, searched, and checked against
the agency. He calibrates the fidelity of the forgery to how adversarially it
will be examined. That is the same reasoning
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|the forensic method]] applies to
*detecting* fabrications, pointed the other way, and it is the reason this
page is filed as a concept and not as a line on a timeline.

**The context brackets it.** The request lands while Annie is on her way into
a counselling appointment; the document is for the parents who have her on a
compliance regime; and the same thread carries a $60 order twenty minutes
later. What is being manufactured is not a document — it is the appearance of
compliance, so the non-compliance can continue. Dan builds the cover for the
supply he is providing, in one afternoon, for the same person.

## Why this matters more than one fake PDF

Three days later, the entire
[[wiki/timeline/events/august-2026-morgantown-call|August 16–19 window]] runs
on documentary authority. Dan's position throughout is *"I don't need to lie.
I have proof of all of this"* and *"If someone wants to call me a liar they
can deal with the receipts."* His threatened instrument is a recording, and
his stated grievance is being disbelieved. He is asking Annie's parents — the
audience of the forgery — to accept a document at face value, forty-eight
hours after building one for the express purpose of being accepted at face
value by those same people.

He does not appear to notice the collision, and the record contains no
acknowledgment of it.

There is a narrower consequence for this wiki specifically. Much of what the
corpus treats as hard evidence is exported, screenshotted or otherwise
image-mediated — the campaign of August 17–19 includes **19 outbound
attachments** in three days, and the wiki has never seen any of them. The
existence of a demonstrated forgery capability does not make those
attachments suspect. It does mean that **an image in this corpus is not
self-authenticating**, and that where a claim rests on a screenshot nobody in
the wiki has opened, the page should say so rather than treat the screenshot's
existence as corroboration.

## The distinction worth keeping

This is not the same act as
[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-handed-mirror|handing someone a dossier about
themselves]], and collapsing them would be a mistake. That is a true document
delivered for effect. This is a false document delivered to defeat a check.
The overlap is the *skill* — assembling something that reads as institutional
— and the difference is the truth value, which is the whole of the moral
distance between them.

It is also not identical to the false-send of August 18, though the two are
the same year and the same instinct: there, Dan fabricated an **event** (*"i
did"* / *"it wasn't actually sent"*) to elicit a reaction. Here he fabricates
an **object** to defeat an inspection. Together they establish that
deliberate deception is available to Dan as an instrument, deployed knowingly,
and — in the drug-test case — deployed on request rather than in anger, which
is the more informative of the two.

## Gaps

- **The artifact itself is not in `raw/`.** Three images were exchanged
  (13:17, 14:08, 14:11) and none is in the corpus. Recovering them would
  settle how good it actually was.
- **Was it used?** Nothing in the record says whether Annie showed it to her
  parents, or whether it worked. Her parents were still requiring proof of
  compliance in the days after.
- **`"I can do these real good"` implies practice.** No other instance is
  documented. A term search for other institutional artifacts — pay stubs,
  letters, results, verifications — has not been run.
- **What tool.** "AI" is Annie's word. Dan's other documented image work runs
  through his ordinary model access ([[wiki/self/concepts/claude]],
  [[wiki/self/concepts/gemini]]); the iteration pattern here — one change per
  round, waiting on each render — is consistent with an image model rather
  than a document editor, but nothing states it.

## What would prove this page wrong

- Evidence that the request was a joke both parties understood as one. The
  emoji at the end of Annie's message admits that reading; the forty minutes
  of error-correction that follow, and *"if you're sending it we need to do a
  lot more work here,"* are what rule it out. A message showing the file was
  never finished or never delivered would narrow the claim from *fabricated*
  to *attempted*.
