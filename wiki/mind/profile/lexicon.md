# Dan's Bespoke Lexicon

A twelve-category phrase generator, built at Dan's request by an AI session
and pasted into this wiki verbatim by operator instruction. It is not a
corpus finding in the usual sense — nobody mined a message dump for it — but
it is still primary evidence of something real: a private register,
deliberately engineered and named ("v1.0," implying iteration), built almost
entirely around [[wiki/people/ally-lubin|Ally]] and meant for active,
repeated use rather than one-off cleverness. The generating session
describes its own method precisely: **elevated register + semantic
displacement + theatrical disproportion + deadpan authority + mythic/
forensic weirdness** — official, evidentiary, tribunal-grade language applied
to something that does not remotely warrant it, with the gap between the
machinery's weight and the triviality of its subject supplying the joke.

## What it actually is

Twelve categories, each a closed vocabulary list plus example sentences,
covering the full emotional range a couple's private idiom needs: beauty
(I–II), enthusiasm (III), praise (IV), disgust (V), stupidity aimed outward
and inward (VI–VII), absurdity (VIII), embarrassment (IX), affectionate
insult (X), and — the two sections the source explicitly flags as the ones
worth keeping — a "Mythological Engine" (XI) that manufactures evidence a
thing has become legendary, and a "Core Slang Generator" (XII) that names
the underlying combinatorial rule directly: **[Official authority] +
[absurd claim]**. Pick an authority (historians, insurers, the tribunal, the
scientific community, the bloodline) and a claim (has exceeded
specifications, cannot be insured, has acquired legendary status,
is not currently understood by science), and the generator produces a new
sentence indefinitely without needing a new joke each time — "the scientific
community has classified Ally as an anomalous concentration of resplendence"
and "Lloyd's has declined to insure your face" are the source's own worked
examples. This is a formula, not a list, which is exactly what the source
means by "the part I'd actually keep."

## What it reveals: the forensic register has two jobs, not one

[[wiki/mind/profile/linguistic-profile]] names Dan's signature idiom
**"forensic intimacy"** — clinical detachment fused with raw personal
confession, present since 2009 and used across the corpus for crisis
analysis, self-diagnosis, and adjudicating relationships. This lexicon is
evidence the same apparatus has a second, previously undocumented job:
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]]'s procedural default — cite the
evidence, invoke an authority, deliver a finding — is not reserved for
threat and anomaly. It is the mechanism of the compliment. "I have reviewed
the available evidence and unfortunately you're gorgeous" is structurally
identical to a forensic-method entry: evidence reviewed, conclusion
rendered, tone withheld — the only thing that has changed is the subject
under investigation. The corpus elsewhere reads the forensic default as a
survival tool turned inward on his own psyche
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/ancestral-dialectic]]); here the same tool is turned
outward, deliberately, as flirtation.

**This complicates, rather than confirms, [[wiki/mind/profile/voice-modes]].**
That page's Affectionate mode is described as a *drop* of the defensive
front — sarcasm and "cold, intellectualizing phrasing" suppressed so
sincerity can stand uncushioned. Categories I and II of this lexicon are
addressed directly to Ally as sincere compliments, and they do the opposite:
the intellectualizing register is not suppressed by the affection, it *is*
the affection's container. "She has rendered ordinary adjectives inadequate"
is not sincerity-with-the-armor-off; it is maximum armor, deployed in
service of sincerity rather than against it. Read against Voice Modes'
own description of Playful ("profanity here reads as affectionate, not
harsh") this lexicon looks less like a violation of the Affectionate/Playful
split and more like evidence the two fuse for this specific relationship:
the deadpan-authority delivery is what makes the compliment land as
*his* voice rather than a generic one, which is itself an intimacy move —
performed sincerity as opposed to bare sincerity, and no less real for being
performed. Category X ("Affectionate Insults") names this fusion directly:
"the sweet spot where love + mock condemnation overlap."

## The taste-signaling angle

The elevated diction — *ontologically gorgeous*, *an affront to ordinary
beauty*, *aesthetic felony*, *administratively difficult* — is not
incidental to the joke; it is [[wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric]]'s
anti-normie aesthetic commitment running on a different vocabulary than
usual. The cool metric documents Dan rejecting inherited, unexamined taste
across music, politics, and media in favor of the deliberately constructed;
this lexicon shows the identical move applied to the smallest unit of
private speech — even "you're pretty" gets engineered rather than said
plainly, because the plain version would be the "normie" one.

## Gaps

- **No usage evidence yet.** A targeted search of the general message
  corpus for several of the lexicon's most distinctive phrases —
  *resplendent*, *administratively*, *the tribunal*, *aesthetic felony*,
  *anomalous concentration* — returned no hits at the time this page was
  written. Consistent with "v1.0": this reads as a freshly commissioned tool
  rather than a documented practice, and the page should be revisited once
  (or if) deployment shows up in the record, to see whether the generator is
  actually used or stays a one-time artifact.
- **No date or model identity on the generating session.** The capture
  gives no timestamp beyond when it was pasted into this wiki, and the
  generating AI's identity is not stated in the paste itself.
- **Whether earlier versions exist is unknown.** The "v1.0" label implies
  iteration or a planned v2; nothing in the corpus currently documents a
  prior version.
