# The Serial Monogamist

Dan has been continuously inside a long-term relationship for roughly
seventeen of the last seventeen years, with the total time unattached
measurable in weeks rather than months or years
([[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]]); the corpus's only completed
exit from a long bond was a same-week transfer to a successor, not a period
of being single ([[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]]); and his own
stated theory of what he looks for in a partner is, wherever the record can
check it, a constructed specification rather than a discovered preference.
Dan has almost no adult lived experience of the thing this page is about —
single life as an actual, occupied state rather than a gap between
transfers — and his account of himself as a dater is built entirely without
that experience to check it against.

## The rule, stated so it can be broken

> Whenever the corpus can check a claim Dan makes about his own dating
> pattern or what draws him to a woman against the documented record, the
> claim resolves to either an engineered specification or an allocation
> policy over an arrangement he authored — never to an organic account of a
> felt type arrived at by trial. And whenever the corpus can check whether
> "single" is a state Dan has actually occupied, the record shows a
> transfer completed before the vacancy could be examined, not an interval
> he lived inside.

## Single life does not appear in the record as a state

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] establishes the header fact:
since [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] in November 2009, the pair-bond
slot has never gone empty for longer than roughly seven weeks, and that one
long vacancy (the June 1, 2026 severance) ended in reentanglement with the
same partner rather than in a documented period of dating around or being
single. The corpus's account of the earlier transition is sharper still.
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/bond-switch-2015]] dates the Alexis-to-Annie handoff
to inside a single calendar month, and the wiki's own reading is explicit
that this was not a gap coped with — **the replacement was sourced before
the vacancy occurred.** There is no week in the documented adult record
where Dan is dating, single, or between relationships in the ordinary
sense of the phrase. The slot is occupied, or it is in the process of
being re-occupied by someone already identified.

This matters for a page about "perspectives on single life" because it
means Dan's opinions on the subject, wherever they appear, are not
field reports. [[wiki/mind/profile/enneagram-5w4]] supplies the mechanism
that predicts this without needing an opinion from him at all: an
sx-dominant instinctual stack **"organises life around one relationship at
maximum voltage, not many."** The prediction that single-and-diffuse would
feel like a missing organizing structure, not a neutral default, is
consistent with a record that shows the position being refilled at the
first opportunity every time it has come open, and it is the reading this
page adopts rather than treating the seventeen years as evidence of a
stated preference for monogamy examined and chosen.

## The self-theory, and what happens when it is checked

The corpus contains exactly one first-person statement of Dan's own theory
of his romantic pattern, offered to a third party with nothing riding on
the answer: *"i'm a serial monogamist so i've only been with a few girls,
and they all fit a very specific type"* (2019-08-17, 22:26). Two things
about the timing matter. It is the only instance of the phrase anywhere in
217,573 messages, and it lands six weeks before the [[wiki/people/kelly-johansson|Kelly
Johansson]] run and two months before the filmed MMF with
[[wiki/people/bryan-5088682461|Bryan]] — the most sexually open documented
window of his adult life. [[wiki/mind/psychosexual/arrangement-history]]
runs the corpus-wide check this deserves: `monogam` appears 7 times and
`faithful` 3 times across the entire message record, against 304
camming-signal tokens and 107 non-monogamy tokens from the same corpus.
**The vocabulary of commitment is nearly absent from a record in which the
vocabulary of arrangement is abundant.** That page's own conclusion is the
one this page adopts: *"openness was never the requirement here —
authorship was."* Applied to the self-theory, "serial monogamist" is best
read not as a description Dan checked against his behavior, but as the
label the continuous-occupancy pattern earns almost by definition — one
partner at a time, no matter how open the arrangement inside that
partnership becomes. It describes the slot, not the sexual practice, and
the corpus shows Dan using the word at the exact moment the practice was
furthest from what the word ordinarily means.

The "very specific type" half of the sentence has its own test, and it
also does not survive contact with the record as a claim about organic
preference. [[wiki/mind/concepts/erotic-architecture]] documents a literal
specification sheet — roughly twenty physical attributes, each scored on a
1–10 scale, hair color given as a hex range, a five-item aesthetic profile
scoring named archetypes by intensity, and a rejected trait (*Tomboy
Femininity*, scored 2) named explicitly rather than simply omitted. Whether
or not the document names a specific person, its existence answers what
kind of claim "a very specific type" is: not a felt preference discovered
through experience, but a quantified target constructed with the same
precision the corpus applies to message counts and chemical dosing.
[[wiki/people/ally-lubin-cognitive-profile]] supplies the closest thing the
record has to Dan stating an organic attraction in his own words — the
2026 love letter's line about her *"ability to spot trends"* and *"spotting
slang and memes that I won't find out about for like 3 more months"* — and
even that sentence, on the page's own reading, describes a quality he
**prices**, not a feeling he simply has. Both documents belong to the same
person and the same habit: nothing in the record of what attracts Dan is
left to run on unexamined default.

## Rescue is the one thing tried, and it is a transfer

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-rescue-premise]] is the member most directly
relevant to a "perspective on single life," because it tests the specific
premise that being extracted from a relationship — landing, if only
briefly, in the unattached state this page is about — would help. The
corpus supplies a controlled instance to test it against: the corpus's
**only completed exit from a long relationship** is the one this page has
already named, and it did not produce single life even briefly. It
produced a transfer, completed inside a week, to a bond that then ran for
the next decade. That page's own framing is the sharpest available
statement of what this means for anyone hoping a rescue would work: *"somebody
comes and gets me out of this"* is *"the single most instantiated move in
Dan's romantic record,"* with *"a sample size of one completed instance,"*
and that instance's cost was the entire following decade. A system built
this way does not distribute its load into a period of being single when
the primary bond fails; per [[wiki/mind/synthesis/single-channel]]'s
finding, it relocates the whole of the load onto whichever channel is
nearest. Single life, on this evidence, is not a state Dan's architecture
is built to pass through. It is the one outcome every documented transition
in the record has been engineered to avoid.

## The pattern is older than any adult relationship

[[wiki/timeline/events/bald-eagle-cummings]] supplies the earliest data
point in the corpus and it changes how everything above should be read.
Describing his seventh-grade girlfriend — by his own account the prettiest
girl in the class — Dan names the shape of the story himself: *"my first
experience with something i would go through many times — somehow getting
a girl way above my level to date me."* That sentence is dated to
roughly 2000–2001, a full eight years before the earliest bond
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] can document. **Dan had a named
theory of his own dating pattern before he had a documented adult dating
history to build one from.** Every later instance — the "very specific
type" claim, the engineered ideal-face document, the mismatch pattern
visible across the corpus — is therefore better read as a repetition of a
self-narrated template laid down at twelve than as an independent
conclusion each adult relationship separately arrived at.

## Falsifiers

1. **A documented period of Dan being genuinely single** — dating multiple
   people without a primary bond, or simply unattached and reporting on the
   experience — lasting longer than roughly six months, at any point since
   2009. None has been found in this pass.
2. **A first-person account of attraction to a specific person that does
   not resolve to a priced, specified, or engineered quality** when checked
   against the surrounding record — a stated preference that survives being
   tested rather than one this page's members already show collapsing under
   inspection.
3. **A documented instance of Dan expressing relief, contentment, or
   positive self-report about being unattached**, comparable in register to
   the Fran-vigil "got good closure" sentence
   ([[wiki/mind/concepts/acquisition-drive]]) but about single life rather
   than about a finished project. The corpus has not been searched
   specifically for this.

## Predictions

1. If a future export ever documents Dan genuinely single for a sustained
   period, the corpus should show him treating it as an anomaly requiring
   resolution — actively working to end it — rather than as a neutral or
   preferred state, consistent with the sx-dominant mechanism this page
   reasons from.
2. Any future first-person statement of "what I look for" will be
   checkable against a document, a habit, or a specification, the same way
   the ideal-face file checks the "very specific type" claim, rather than
   standing as an unexamined preference.
3. The next documented transition out of a long bond, if one occurs, will
   show a successor identified before or immediately upon the vacancy,
   consistent with both documented transitions to date (2015, 2026).

## Gaps

- **The 2007–2009 window is undocumented**, the same gap
  [[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-unbroken-bond]] already flags — whether Dan
  experienced any period of being single between high school and the
  Alexis relationship is unknown, and it is the single biggest hole in this
  page's central claim.
- **Whether the ideal-face document names a specific person is unresolved.**
  [[wiki/mind/concepts/erotic-architecture]] treats it as generic by
  construction and draws no line from it to anyone in the corpus; if it
  does encode a real person, this page's "engineered rather than organic"
  reading would need to be checked against whether the specification was
  built to match someone already met rather than to search for someone not
  yet found.
- **No primary source states, in Dan's own words, why "single" is avoided**
  rather than merely never occurring. Every mechanism this page cites —
  the sx-dominant stack, the Irreversibility Firewall's no-delete rule, the
  no-failover architecture — is an outside reading of the pattern, not his
  stated account of it.
- **The self-theory quote is n=1.** Whether "serial monogamist... a very
  specific type" was said again, in different words, at other points in the
  eleven-year corpus has not been checked systematically; a
  `bin/mine-messages` pass for self-description language around
  relationships and dating would either strengthen or complicate this
  page's reading of the one instance found.
