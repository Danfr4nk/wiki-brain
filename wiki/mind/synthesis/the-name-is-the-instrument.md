# The Name Is the Instrument

He never names anything neutrally. Pets, handles, AI personas, and political identities all carry deliberately loaded names — and the name does work in the world, reading the room so he doesn't have to.

The rule, stated plainly: every entity he names and keeps carries a loaded, provocative, or ironic namesake, and the name functions as an instrument — a social tripwire that sorts its audience by their reaction — rather than as a label.

Dan's own three-name set, stated in his own words — "Milo, Gabe, Max," all drawn from people he describes as "the most reprehensible people society is throwing up" — extends the pattern to AI personas and corroborates it: he is explicit that the point isn't admiration, it's diagnostic.

## Controls and counterexamples

The honest control the corpus does not yet supply is a neutrally named, kept entity with no tripwire function — a dog named Biscuit, kept without irony. Its absence is itself the current state of the evidence; the rule has not yet met a counterexample, and the page will carry one if it arrives rather than absorbing it.

The accumulation finding is load-bearing: the Milo name has gained functions over time without shedding the sorting one. Loaded names don't wear out in this system; they accrete. That is what makes the rule a claim about an instrument rather than a habit — instruments get more uses, habits get stale.

## Mechanism, labeled

Trust at the 9th percentile with suspicion-of-motive at 1.96x baseline ([[wiki/mind/profile/big-five-psychometrics]], corpus-measured). A tripwire name outsources the trust judgment: the stranger's reaction to the name does the sorting that his own trust faculty cannot do cheaply. Confidence: medium — the Trust-9 link is inference, not a corpus claim, and is stated as such per the trait-filter rule. It is not load-bearing: the rule stands on the four members with or without it.

## Prediction

The next entity he names and keeps — pet, AI persona, project alias, handle — will carry a loaded, provocative, or ironic namesake rather than a neutral one. Falsifier of the strong form: a neutrally named, kept entity with no tripwire function. Weaker break: a loaded name adopted with no observable sorting use within 90 days.
