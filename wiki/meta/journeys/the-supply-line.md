# The Supply Line

Two things move through this record that look unrelated until they are traced
together: money and drugs. Read separately, one is a family's inheritance
history and the other is an addiction history. Read as one route, they are
the same shape — capital arrives in a lump from a source that held it for
years, passes through a switchboard, and drains out through a metered flow
that never lets the lump become a balance. This journey walks that route
start to finish, using only what the corpus's own pages already establish.

## The source: a lump held for decades

[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran Whyel Coldren]] — Dan's maternal
great-grandmother, dead since 2018 — is not herself a source of drug money.
She is the source of the *pattern's largest lump*: an Ira-estate trust her
family held for decades, released in 2020 as a $144,069.31 distribution — the
single largest capital arrival documented anywhere in Dan's biography. She
taught him to read, funded his production equipment, and employed him at the
end of her life; the money that reaches him after her death is the family's
oldest capital, arriving all at once rather than as income.

## The route: [[wiki/mind/synthesis/estate-money-spine|the money spine]]

The spine traces where that lump — and every other capital event around it,
Rick's business income, the 2020 market run, the eventual sale of the family
house — actually goes. Its governing finding, already published, is that
**capital arrives in lumps and exits through standing drains**: nothing in
the record converts into an asset that holds. The largest single documented
drain the spine identifies is $119,000–$123,000 in payment-app transfers to
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] — roughly the size of the entire estate
distribution, gone the same way it came, in constant metered flow rather
than one event.

## The switchboard: [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]]

Dan's mother is not a passive conduit. The estate check was deposited through
her; she facilitated the Cash App transfers the supply purchases ran on; and
in 2018 she was the destination of the largest capital movement recorded
running the *other* direction — roughly $14,000 from Dan to her, drawn
against an estate that had not yet even distributed. She is the one node in
the spine capital moves through in both directions.

## The drain: [[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network|the supply network]]

On the outbound side, the money becomes drugs. The network names its own
suppliers by era — family-internal provision in 2015–16, a deep 2018 cycle,
the 2026 terminal phase's daily house calls — and its own governing finding
is the mirror of the money spine's: **the architecture persists because the
want is not negotiable, so it cannot be left to one supplier.** The network's
last transaction, on the record, is not a dramatic rupture but an ordinary
failure — a $60 handoff that was never completed — and the relationship it
ran through ended inside the five hours that failure opened.

## The product: [[wiki/health/cocaine|cocaine]]

Named directly on its own page as "the primary product moving through the
supply network across its entire documented history" — from the 2015
family-internal provision through the 2018 deep cycle to the 2026 daily
calls. The cocaine page is where the demand side of the network is
quantified; the supply-network page is where the route is named. Together
they close the loop the estate money opened: a lump of inherited capital,
arriving once, financing a product that has to be bought again every day.

## Where the two roles fuse: [[wiki/people/tom|Tom]]

Tom's own page states his dual function in one sentence: he functions
"simultaneously as anchor and supply line." He is Dan's clearest documented
case of safe, lateral, non-paternal attachment — and also the strip source
who supplied Suboxone, mushrooms, cocaine and klonopin for most of the
record. The friendship's own page documents its collapse in spring 2026
under exactly the strain this journey traces: the supply-line role failing is
what the anchor role could not survive. That is the whole route in one
person — which is why this journey ends here rather than on a page about
money or a page about drugs alone.

## What would falsify this

If a future ingest found a capital arrival in this record that *did*
accumulate — sat in an account, appreciated, was never drawn down against a
standing drain — that would be a counter-instance the supply-line reading
would have to absorb. None is currently on any page in this route.
