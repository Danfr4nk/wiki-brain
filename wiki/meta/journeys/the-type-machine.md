# The Type Machine

Dan Frank has been running instruments over himself since at least July 2013
— a full decade before any AI system existed to do it for him. This journey
follows that practice from its first dated instance to its current, largest
form: this wiki itself.

## Where it starts: a five-day rebound gets typed

[[wiki/people/franki-faris|Franki Faris]] is a five-day relationship from the
summer of 2013 that left almost no independent trace — no message corpus, no
contact after 2013, nothing. What it did leave is a batch of self-typology
emails Dan sent himself between July 9 and July 31, 2013, typing Franki as an
ESFP "Performer" against [[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]]'s INFP/ISFP
"Idealist" — an attempt, in his own later framing, to "reboot" his identity
around her opposite. The same batch carries something the Franki relationship
itself has nothing to do with: his own earliest documented INTP
self-identification.

## The type that stuck

[[wiki/mind/profile/intp]] traces that self-identification to its actual
first dated instance: a July 2013 message to a friend — *"as an INTP and
heavily introverted, logic-based dude...i can't see myself flourishing in
AA/NA"* — using the type as a credential in an argument against group-based
recovery, not as a description of an inner state. That distinction matters:
the type was deployed instrumentally from its very first appearance, which
the page itself notes is "the pattern's origin rather than an exception to
it." Every AI-assisted typology exercise that follows over the next decade
repeats the same move at greater scale.

## The control case: typed, then found untyped

[[wiki/people/alexis-armel|Alexis]] was typed in the same July 2013 batch —
and it didn't hold. Her own page carries the correction: two independent
2026-08-21 passes concluded she has no type at all, a finding that could only
be made once the datum stopped living only on a five-day rebound's page and
was carried on her own page as well. Alexis is already the corpus's control
case for attachment — six years next to Dan without the deep attachment
system this wiki otherwise documents in catastrophic detail ever fully
activating. Here she is a second kind of control case: the one person typed
early in the record whom the apparatus, on its own later re-examination,
could not actually type.

## The apparatus, industrialized

[[wiki/mind/synthesis/the-commissioned-self]] picks the practice up a decade
later and names what it had quietly become: not an unusual interest in
personality tests, but **"an unusual amount of instrumentation."** Seven
independent frameworks have been run over him — MBTI with function
percentages, Enneagram with wing and instinct stack, Big Five/Big30 with a
SLOAN code, a personality-disorder vector, Socionics, Attitudinal Psyche, a
moral alignment — plus a stylometric analysis, a deviance audit against a
demographic baseline, and a composite voice model. The page's own governing
line: *"Dan knows himself the way he knows a stranger: by running an
instrument over the evidence and reading the output."* The 2013 batch is not
an outlier next to this. It is the prototype.

## Where the apparatus points now

[[wiki/self/concepts/wiki-brain|The wiki-brain]] — this repository — closes
the journey by naming itself as a member of its own subject matter. Its own
frontmatter carries a `caused-by` edge into the-commissioned-self stating
plainly: **"This repository is the current and largest instance of a
self-measurement apparatus that already ran four generations deep — and the
first whose readings are published where other instruments will read them
back."** A 2013 email batch typing a five-day rebound and a 438-plus-page
second brain reasoning across typed connections are, on this reading, the
same practice at two different scales, thirteen years apart.

## What would falsify this

If a typology exercise were ever found in the record that Dan ran on someone
*other than himself or a partner, for a reason unconnected to explaining or
managing his own behavior*, that would be a counter-instance — evidence the
instrumentation habit is general curiosity rather than self-directed
apparatus-building. None of the five stops on this journey supply one; every
instance here either types Dan directly or types a partner in service of
explaining Dan's own conduct toward her.

**Gaps:** this journey states no new date, figure or typology result — every
claim above already lives on one of its five stops. What it adds is the
sequence, which none of the five pages states end to end on its own.
