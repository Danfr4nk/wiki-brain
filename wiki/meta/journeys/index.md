# journeys — index

Curated, narrated reading paths through several existing pages at once —
`page_type: journey` (`STYLE_GUIDE.md`). Each one states a thesis in prose and
walks its stops in order; none adds a new fact or figure that isn't already
published on the page it cites.

- [[wiki/meta/journeys/the-supply-line]] — money and drugs run the same
  route: a lump arrives from a source held for decades, passes through one
  switchboard, and drains out through a metered flow that never lets it
  become a balance. 6 stops.
- [[wiki/meta/journeys/the-instrumented-channel]] — five instruments built to
  measure one high-volume channel, in the order they were built, each one a
  correction or a generalization of the one before it. 5 stops.
- [[wiki/meta/journeys/the-type-machine]] — a self-typology habit documented
  from its first dated instance in July 2013 to its current, largest form:
  this wiki. 5 stops.
