# Twitter / X — 2022

**The year the position is settled enough to be turned on his own side, and the
year the method costs him something in public.** 2022 is the account's busiest
since 2013 — 158 originals — and it is the first year the writing is
consistently *argued* rather than posted: numbered theories, corrections,
forecasts he returns to score, and one three-day sequence in which he ends up
agreeing with people he despises and says so.

Much of this year was already mined into other pages. What this page adds is
the shape it has as a year.

## The Paul Pelosi sequence: the method, and its bill

Between **29 October and 2 November**, Dan refused to accept the account of the
Paul Pelosi attack. `wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism` already
carries the finding. What belongs here is what it cost him, in his own words:

> *"It super sucks being (as far as I can tell) the only person not buying the
> Paul pelosi story who isn't a groyper maga shitlord."*
> *"And I'm just disgusted that sides have been drawn and on this one single
> issue, I find myself with the most vile and hilariously wrong and morally
> bankrupt people in media…"*
> *"But fuck dude.. I'm sorry I do not believe several critical details of the
> story and those details determine whether or not this was political terrorism
> or not that. Not speculating about what DID happen but I can't…"*
> *"What actually happened? I have absolutely zero idea."*

He also, in the middle of it, tells his followers to *"plz still vote blue
everyone."*

**This is the whole disposition in one sequence, including the part that
usually stays theoretical.** He detects the anomaly, refuses the consensus,
declines to substitute a story of his own, notices that the refusal has put him
in the worst possible company, states that it disgusts him — and does not move.
The corpus argues in several places that anomaly detection outranks tribe for
Dan. Here the tribe is one he had adopted two years earlier and is actively
campaigning for, the stakes are two weeks before a midterm, and the cost is
being publicly indistinguishable from people he calls morally bankrupt. He pays
it.

## Disciplining his own side, fourteen months in

The 2020 declaration policed the boundary outward. By 2022 he is doing it
inward, at the pipeline itself.

On **22 February**, on Hasan Piker — one of the four channels
`wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn` names as the mechanism of his conversion:

> *"I love Hasan but his take on Russia is either purposefully antagonistic at
> the expense of his intellectual credibility or astonishingly naive in the
> face of overwhelming contradictory evidence."*
> *"I shudder to think of a future where the most high-profile and promising
> ambassador of socialism slides into the jimmy dore / greenwald category of
> empty-headed reactionary grifterism."*

And on **27 February**, to the left generally: *"Message to fellow leftists:
this is not the hill to die on. Your criticism of hypocrisy in US foreign
policy is correct. Unfortunately, this is what makes it such an effective tool
for Russian agitprop."*

The pattern the earlier years establish — that he leaves affiliations when the
people carrying them become the argument against them — is visible here at an
earlier stage than any previous instance. He is not leaving. He is trying to
prevent the conditions that would make him leave, which no earlier affiliation
in the archive got from him.

## He is waiting tables, and he tells it as somebody else's story

**30 June**:

> *"Wheels are falling waiting tables but i catch a table with a dude giving
> unusually good takes all night. Tell him that if he had a podcast I would
> listen and he tells me 'I do have a podcast.'"*
> *"For what it's worth there could be no more organic or meaningful praise /
> validation of talent for a broadcaster than being so naturally entertaining
> off the clock that your waiter (who has given you barely mediocre service)…"*

He is thirty-three, and the fact of the job arrives in a subordinate clause
inside an anecdote about how good a stranger's conversation was. The 2016 page
named this: **employment appears on this account only as material, never as a
fact stated.** Six years and at least two jobs later, unchanged.

## Advising Opie on his career

On **18 July**, the three-tweet thread `wiki/interests/opie-and-anthony` is
built on. The first two are the radicalisation account. The third is not
analysis at all:

> *"i think you're missing a huge opportunity to be embraced among the
> 'dirtbag left' class of shows like chapo trap house, cumtown (RIP) and
> streetfight radio by going full progressive."*

Ten years after the internship application, eight after the unpaid clip work,
he is offering the host a repositioning strategy — unprompted, publicly, to a
man he had written off in 2018 with *"opie sucks though"*. The relationship to
that show does not resolve; it just keeps changing register.

## Forecasting as a practice, with a scoreboard

2022 is where a habit becomes a method: he makes **dated, falsifiable public
predictions and comes back to them.**

- **12 April** — a full 2022 Senate map built on yapms: *"Basically all eyes on PA/GA/AZ/WI again this cycle"*
- **13 April, 7 December** — PredictIt prices quoted as evidence (*"DeSantis now +15 on Trump (42¢/27¢)"*)
- **24 August** — a forecast held against contrary news: *"I seriously hope I'm just being a cynical grouch but neither the Kansas ballot initiative, Ryan winning #NY19 nor the post-Dobbs polling momentum changes my forecast for house control"*
- **26 October** — a local-knowledge prediction: *"I know PA voters…and let me tell you, none of them will see the Fetterman situation with any empathy"*
- **14 November** — the return: *"We'll see if I've got my finger on the pulse of the ever unpredictable MAGA movement…"*
- **15 December** — seven numbered theories about a Trump announcement, each with its reasoning, published before the event

**This belongs to `wiki/meta/testimony-veracity` as much as to politics.** That
ledger exists because most of Dan's first-person claims are unfalsifiable; here
is a body of claims he made *deliberately* falsifiable, dated, in public, with
his reasoning attached and a stated intention to check. It is the best
available material for scoring his calibration, and none of it is in the ledger
yet.

## Two encounters with machines and one with a livestream

**8 September**, three months before ChatGPT: he runs a supposedly
historically-accurate image of Jesus through DALL·E 2 for three variations,
gets back the conventional Western portrayal, records the screen, and asks
whether it is an artefact of the code — *"Is this the new jesus toast?
@openai"*. `wiki/self/concepts/chatgpt` already carries this as the
corroboration of hands-on pre-ChatGPT model use. What is worth adding is the
*form*: an anomaly, an experiment, a recording, and a question addressed to the
people who would know. The same four steps as the terminal-lucidity thread of
2018, on a completely different subject.

**15 May**, on watching the Buffalo shooter's livestream:

> *"the video from the livestreamed mass shooting in Buffalo today was
> profoundly disturbing and haunting, even to a very-online millennial with a
> high tolerance to NSFL content"*
> *"I've played a fucking ton of FPS games and was not sure how much tolerance
> I would have to seeing POV violence…the minutiae and relationship to the
> 'human element' was something completely foreign to any experience"*

That is `wiki/mind/synthesis/millennial-digital-witness` at its darkest: he
reports his own reaction as **data about the limits of media saturation**,
explicitly benchmarked against the desensitisation he expected from games and
found insufficient. The disposition that produced the Balloon Boy jokes in 2009
is here reporting on where it breaks.

## Texture

handle as the repository this wiki lives in — with published videos, one
removed by moderation (7 October) and a week on the platform described as
*"fucking horrifying"* (21 October). *Rise and Fall of the Third Reich* read
with a scoring rubric (27 December: *"keeping track of all the times that it is
made clear that the nazi ideology was objectively not left-wing"*). A
preference for Scribd over Audible stated *"apropos of nothing"* (12 November).
Project Veritas apparently naming him (9 December): *"bring it on. This is
going to be awesome."* And **1 November**, his birthday: *"My last minute being
33"*, then *"Maybe the clock will never change to 12."*

On **24 September**, the fourteenth anniversary: *"14 years of pointless
nothingness but at least I got the @danfrank handle"* — which, with the 2010
counters, is the third independent confirmation of a September 2008 account
creation.

## What would change this reading

The forecasting claim is the one with the most riding on it and it is
incomplete: this page identifies the practice but does not score it. Somebody
should — the predictions are dated and the outcomes are public, so the work is
available and the result could go either way. The waiting-tables reading treats
one clause as a fact about the year; no employer, dates or duration are
anywhere in the corpus. And the Pelosi sequence is interpreted here as method
holding under cost, which is the generous reading; the ungenerous one is that
he was wrong and stubborn about it for four days, and the corpus cannot settle
which, because the underlying question was never resolved in public either.
