# Twitter / X — 2013

**The year the account becomes a music project's feed, and the year the record
itself changes underneath that.** From mid-August onward the page is a
continuous stream of releases under the name **SLOPPP** — roughly thirty mixes
and original tracks between 13 August and 26 December, better than one every
four days — plus SoundCloud play counts, gear, hashtags and requests for
critique.

It is also the year the six-year Alexis relationship breaks and reassembles
inside about six weeks, with another person documented in the gap; the year he
appears to relocate from Manhattan to Western Pennsylvania; and the year he
posts approvingly of a **critique of the New Atheism** he was evangelising two
years earlier.

## First, the thing that will mislead everyone who reads this page

**The apparent explosion of music activity in mid-August is, in large part, the
archive changing.** The Coverage table below is the whole warning: 199 of this
page's 346 rows come from the operator spreadsheet, which is complete only
**from 2013-08-17**, and everything before that date is a live scrape truncated
to ten results per month-bounded query.

So the page shows a sparse, jokey, non-musical January-to-July and a dense,
musical August-to-December, and **that shape is at least partly manufactured by
where one source stops and the other starts.** The transition date in the data
(17 August) and the apparent transition in the subject matter are within a week
of each other, which is exactly the coincidence that should stop a reader
concluding anything.

**What can honestly be said about when SLOPPP starts:** the earliest trace on
this page is **31 July 2013**, inside a truncated month-end sample, and it is
already casual about the practice — *"twice now i've had some (unintentionally)
played background music while checking latest mix"*. A person "checking latest
mix" in July is not a person who began in August. The project is therefore
**older than this page can date**, and no claim about its start, ramp or
intensity should be drawn from the shape here. A complete export would settle
it; nothing else will.

## SLOPPP, as an operation rather than a hobby

With that caveat, what the complete second half of the year does support is
detailed and worth having in full, because it shows a **practice with
infrastructure**, not a person posting tracks.

**Output.** Named releases include *THE TALK IT OUT MIX*, *THE INTEREST OF
CONFLICT MIX [MOOMBATHON/2STEP]*, *THE CHURCH OF MANNIE FRESH*, *FULL RATCHET
JACKET*, *JESSE PINKMAN'S ROADTRIP, YO*, *SOUNDTRACK TO A STRUGGLE: BACK TO
SCHOOL EDITION*, *helterskelter: race riot music*, *THE KEEPSAKE FOR PETE'S
SAKE MIX*, *THE GREENER GRASS MIX*, *SLOPPPED AND SCREWED: HOODIE WEATHER MIX*,
*TUNE IN, DROP OUT, TURN UP*, *RACK TO THE FUTURE: SEPTEMBER 2013 TRAP MIX
[TRACKLISTED & TIMESTAMPED]*, *THE LOW-END MIX*, *THINK ABOUT IT! THURSDAYS*,
*CUFFED AND CRUISED*, *BULLET SVNDWICH*, *A BRIEF HISTORY OF TIME AND BASS*,
*STARS AND BARS: FLAG OR SWAG?*, *TRICK OR TRAP — MOLLOWEEN 2013*, *TOO TURNT
TO TWERK*, and a two-part *YEARMIX 2013*. Alongside the mixes are **original
tracks** — *All Done*, *Blazin*, *Swisher*, *Snailed It*, *Backwards*, *Betty
Draper* — and official-style reworks of Baauer and 2 Chainz.

**Distribution.** SoundCloud primarily, but also Mixcloud (`thedanfrank`) and
8tracks, with Instagram and Vine used as promotional surfaces. Mixes are
released *"TRACKLISTED & TIMESTAMPED"* and *"FREE DL"* — the conventions of the
scene, correctly observed.

**Audience, self-reported and self-deflated.** On **10 November**: *"just
topped 7k plays on soundcloud. thanks to @alexisarmel for something like 6,000
of those"*. On **8 December**: *"almost to 10k plays."* Roughly ten thousand
plays across some thirty releases in four months, and the first number arrives
with the joke that undercuts it already attached.

**Craft, and a named teacher.** He asks for criticism, from one person, by
name — **15 August**, to `@Yamez1`: *"listen to this for me? I wanna hear what
you think I need to improve"*. `@Yamez1` gets more of his attention than anyone
in the scene (*"must live in Bosnia with all these bombs he's dropping"*,
*"YOU'RE ON SOME DEXTER SHIT WITH THESE CUTS SON"*), and on **10 November** the
gear moves up: *"grabbed this new ns7 with motor platters and I'm f00kin amazed
I ever lived on cheap plastic."* On **23 September** he reads the history:
*"The dense and illustrious history of the 808 sample (and 909)."*

**And he is his own harshest reader, in public.** On 23 September a release
goes out hashtagged *"#overcompensating #poseur #fakefuture"*; on 11 August,
looking at old work, *"Wow I was/am a fucking HACK."* The self-assessment runs
alongside the promotion in the same feed on the same days, which is the shape
`wiki/mind/profile/index` describes as an on/off verdict with no middle
setting — here it is, applied to his own output, hourly.

## Six weeks single, and somebody else in the gap

`wiki/timeline/events/franki-fireworks-day-2013` dates the six-year
relationship's only mid-course rupture to July 2013 and to Alexis's detection
of it. This page supplies the duration and what happened inside it.

| Date | Post | What it fixes |
|---|---|---|
| 2013-08-09 | *"I guess I tweet more when I'm single?"* | he is single, in his own words, in August |
| 2013-08-21 → 08-31 | `@katie_efff` — Blue Star Point Lookout, *"Mountain things"*, a pedicure, Oddball Comedy Fest in Pittsburgh, *"Frogs and Katie Fletcher"* | a named other person, repeatedly, over eleven days |
| 2013-09-07 | *"All smilez with a mysteriously amazing young girl"* / *"My better 1/2."* / *"Mystery girl and everything's okay now"* | something has resolved |
| 2013-09-08 | *"Alexis Armel and a doppelgänger mosquito"* | Alexis is back, named |
| 2013-09-17 onward | *"Noone does 'contemplative' (or perfect) quite like @alexisarmel"*, *"If having a bad bitch was a crime I'd be arrested"* | fully reconciled |

So the break runs from **July to the first week of September** — call it six to
eight weeks — and Katie Fletcher is in it, publicly, in photographs, at a
comedy festival. **This is the only documented interval in the six years where
the relationship is off**, and it is worth holding beside the corpus's account
of Dan's attachment behaviour: the gap is short, it is filled immediately, and
the reconciliation is announced in the language of relief (*"everything's okay
now"*) rather than of decision.

The one thing this page cannot settle is whether the 7 September *"mystery
girl"* is Katie or Alexis. Both readings fit and the next day names Alexis;
neither is asserted here.

## He is in Pennsylvania

From mid-August the geography changes and the page stops being a New York
document: Carnegie Mellon (14 August), Sheetz twice (20 August, 3 September),
Texas Roadhouse (29 August), the Oddball Comedy Festival in Pittsburgh
(31 August), an argument about Syria *"with an idiot in Sheetz"*, and on
**27 September** *"For those curious what the hooplah in uniontown is about
today"* with *"Fucking helicopters and shit"* — filmed, in his hometown.

`wiki/self/location-history` records that address-level data runs out after
January 2011 and does not resume until the 2014 Google export, and names that
window as the corpus's location blind spot. **This page partially fills it**:
whatever his formal address was, Dan was substantially in Fayette County from
August 2013, which is two years earlier than the return the wiki otherwise
narrates. It is presence, not tenancy — the tweets place him there and do not
say he lived there — and that distinction is left open deliberately.

## The atheism turns on itself

2011 was militant movement atheism at full volume. February 2013 still is:
*"You have to make it clear to everyone, including your children, that there is
no god"* (20 February), plus a prediction on the papal succession (*"expect the
church to use popegate as marketing opportunity and rebrand catholicism with
younger, more relatable pope"* — 12 February, which is what happened) and CNN's
*"Another John Paul Miracle Found"* treated as cause for violence.

Then, on **30 July 2013**:

> *"This is a great critique of neo-atheism. 'The Enormity of the Mystery That
> Surrounds Us @ TEDx'"*

*Neo-atheism* is the movement's own term for itself. Five months after telling
people to make sure their children know there is no god, he is publicly
endorsing an argument against the position's style. **The belief does not
change and the affiliation does** — which is the same shape as every other
departure in this corpus: he leaves the tribe, not the conclusion.

## Boston, and reading the theory of the thing he did

On **21 April 2013**, six months after Sandy:

> *"Great article about social media coverage during Boston bombing and the new
> role of journalists"*

He is no longer doing it or noticing it — he is reading analysis of it. Beside
that, he treats the bombing's conspiracy layer with contempt rather than
appetite: *"conspiracy du jour: americans under attack from vicious stoners"*,
and to `@OpieRadio`, *"is this the 'marbles in ass' part of the boston
conspiracy?"* He also flags Limbaugh comparing the suspect to Trayvon Martin.

That is a third data point for
`wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism`'s finding that the
"conspiracy crank" label is contradicted by his own behaviour — during the
American event most productive of live conspiracy theorising in the decade, he
mocks them.

## The register at its worst, and one thing that complicates it

2013 is the peak year for the shock vocabulary: *"fuck gender roles, everyone
walks like a faggot in the snow"* (9 February), *"Spooks and gooks"*
(24 October), *"Kill yourself, make the world a better place"* at his own
sister (14 August), a Hitler/Eva Braun joke at Alexis (22 November), and
*"SUPER USEFUL"* sarcasm at an article about talking to wheelchair users
(25 November).

It is kept for the same reason 2012's is: it is the measurable distance the
2020 turn later covers, and a corpus that deletes it cannot claim the
movement.

**What complicates it** — and this is not a defence, it is a fact on the same
page — is that the same months contain sustained, non-ironic engagement with
Kanye West's *Yeezus* and its racial argument. He builds a mix titled
*helterskelter: race riot music*, tags it `#NewSlaves`, and on **2 September**
writes: *"My new fav thing to do is drive around looking for REALLY white
people and play them the acapella version of 'new slaves' from my car stereo."*
On **31 August**, in Pennsylvania: *"Being a vet does NOT make your racism ok
nor does it make you automatically correct."* And on **25 August**, revisiting
his own past judgement: *"I remembr a 2005 conversation wherein we determined
Kanye to be the most racist person/rapper on earth. We didn't have any idea."*

A man using racial slurs as punchlines in October and driving around
provoking white people with *New Slaves* in September is not two people. He is
one person inside the O&A comedy register whose actual sympathies are
already elsewhere, six years before he has a vocabulary for them.

## Texture, and one small durable thing

The Codecademy badges on **19 February** — *"I just unlocked the 50 points
earned in one day badge"*, then the 75-point badge the same day — are already
carried by `wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch` and `wiki/work/tech/index`,
and belong here as their source: dated, self-taught programming, thirteen years
before the AI work.

Elsewhere: *Arrested Development* season four watched as a reference-spotting
exercise with a written list (26–27 May); *"the only people for me are the mad
ones"* quoted from Kerouac on 10 July and then quoted back at him by Alexis
three weeks later; a cousin, `@franknarcissist`, writing his first cover story
for *The Fader* on 19 November; *"I'm the opposite of apathetic….i'm
pathetic"* (23 March); *"I'm looking for plot holes. I'm looking for terrible
art. I'm looking for the bland, the bizarre, the lazy, the insipid, the
surreal!"* (31 August), which is the corpus's anomaly-detection orientation
stated as an aesthetic; and the unexplained phrase **"panic tax"**, posted
alone on 20 January and again on 13 September, referent unknown.

## What would change this reading

Almost everything above about *timing* is hostage to the source boundary at
17 August. If the pre-August months arrive in full and contain the same volume
of music, the "year the account becomes a music feed" framing is wrong and
SLOPPP simply predates the record. If they arrive and contain none, the framing
is confirmed. This page cannot tell, and says so rather than choosing.

The Pennsylvania relocation is inferred from presence, not from any statement
of address; a lease or a dated message would settle whether he moved or was
visiting for six weeks. And the relationship reconstruction depends on
*"I guess I tweet more when I'm single?"* being literal rather than a joke —
if it is a joke, the only hard facts left are Katie Fletcher's eleven days in
late August and Alexis's reappearance on 8 September.
