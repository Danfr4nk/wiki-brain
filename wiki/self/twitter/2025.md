# Twitter / X — 2025

**Thirteen posts, and the year's finding is which eleven months are missing.**

The account posts nine times in January and February, once on 27 February, and
then **nothing at all until 2 October** — a single music post — and nothing
after. 2025 is the emptiest year on the account since it opened.

## The silence covers the founding of this project

April 2025 is when Dan's documented AI turn begins: the ChatGPT session that
`wiki/self/concepts/chatgpt` treats as the origin of the work, and the start of
the sustained self-analysis that this wiki is the product of. **None of it is
on this account.** The public record goes quiet in February and does not
meaningfully return.

This is the second time it has happened, and the pair is the point.
`wiki/self/twitter/2015` records a five-month blackout across the most
turbulent stretch of his adult life. 2025 is an eleven-month blackout across the
most *generative* one. The feed is not a diary that reflects what is happening;
**it goes quiet in both directions — under pressure and under absorption.**

Anyone reading these nineteen pages as a life record needs that stated plainly:
the two years in which most happened are the two years with least on them, and
the correlation runs the wrong way for the naive reading.

## What the eleven posts contain

The January material is ordinary political commentary of a piece with 2024 —
the Hegseth confirmation hearing (*"He weaseled his way out of answering
everything. Democratic Senators offered few, if any questions of substance"*,
14 January), Republicans *"pretending not to know what Q Anon is"* (16 January),
and a genuinely puzzled post about opposition to conditioning foreign aid
(5 January). On 1 February he posts four times about a plane crash in
Philadelphia going uncovered — *"A plane fell out of the sky onto a residential
neighborhood in a major city and…noting?"*, then *"Update: 3 hours later and
still nothing"*, then tagging both papers. The media-monitoring reflex, intact
and unchanged since Sandy.

Two personal ones matter more than their length.

**7 January**: *"My self-teaching piano lesson tonight is figuring out a bunch
songs from criminally under-appreciated national treasure @NickLutsko."*

`wiki/self/twitter/2016` records the inherited alto sax, clarinet and mandolin,
and his own forecast that *"the amount of time I'm gonna spend becoming mediocre
at them will be crzy."* Nine years later he is teaching himself piano, by ear,
from a comedy musician's songs. The forecast was accurate about the activity and
this page cannot say whether it was accurate about the outcome.

**27 February**: *"Now that I have my title I'm ready to write my memoir 'Other
Things, and Other Things Other than That'."*

The **fourth** memoir-title joke in the archive — after *"GO READ A BOOK OR
SOMETHING"* (2014) and the two of 2017. Four times in eleven years he has
announced a title for an autobiography and written nothing. It is a joke every
time, and it is the same joke, and the thing it is about is a book about
himself. This corpus is what he built instead.

## What would change this reading

The blackout claim depends on 2025 being archived as completely as the export
suggests, and 2016 is proof the export can drop rows. A live-scrape pass over
March–September 2025 is the specific thing that would settle it, and it is worth
running: eleven months missing from the most consequential year in the recent
record is either a real finding or a serious hole, and nothing on this page can
tell which.
