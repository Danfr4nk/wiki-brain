# Twitter / X — 2011

**The year the reasoning style arrives fully formed, and the career goes
backwards.** By the end of 2010 Dan had a Brooklyn apartment, a girlfriend and
a studio job. Three weeks into 2011 he is writing *"the never ending job
hunt"*, and by 28 January he is at *"my marketing internship"* — unpaid. In
October he reports, from the office, *"Correction: I am a dog walker."*

Against that, the thinking is at its most confident and most public. 2011 is
where the argumentative move the corpus later calls the **adversarial
historian** is running at full volume — thirteen years before the year
`wiki/mind/synthesis/twitter-2024-cognitive-state` names it — and it is running
almost entirely on religion and on vertical authority, not yet on economics.

## The move, in its purest specimen: 21 May 2011

Harold Camping had predicted the Rapture for 21 May. On the day, Dan did not
post the obvious joke. He posted this:

> *"RELIGION- WRONG AGAIN."*
> *"the only difference between the 5/21 rapture followers and moderate
> christians is that the former are willing to put a date on the end."*
> *"why is it more conceivable that jesus would return in 1,000 or 10,000
> years, than it is that he would return today?"*

The first line is the provocation. The second and third are the actual
operation: he takes the fringe case everyone is laughing at, identifies the
**only** structural difference between it and the respectable case — the fringe
version is *falsifiable* and the mainstream version is not — and turns the
laughter back on the people doing it. That is the sequence the 2024 synthesis
describes as *model → stress-test → identify contradiction → compress →
provoke*, executed in three tweets at twenty-two years old.

He does it again on **26 February**, to a working pastor, by scripture:

> *"@PastorSamDavis since you're so keen on scripture, what do you think of
> 1 Timothy 2:12?!"* … *"I do not permit a woman to teach or to have authority
> over a man, she must be silent"* … *"stop promoting this Iron Age bullshit
> folklore. Help people because you CAN, not to suck up to your sky daddy."*

Same shape: adopt the opponent's own authority, find where it breaks, publish
the break. **This is the wiki's "find where it breaks" methodology, dated to
2011 and applied to strangers in public.**

## The public atheism has a peak and this is it

`CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED` dates aggressive atheism to 2007. 2010 showed it
surfacing in his writing. 2011 is where it becomes **movement participation**
rather than private position, and the density is unlike any other year on the
account: the bird-and-fish "apocalypse" as *"media sensationalism. you can all
return to your bibles now"* (6 January); *"Big Irrelevant Book of Lies and
Exaggerations"* (23 January); an Epicurean formulation on 22 February (*"an
all-powerful God, who creates faulty Humans, and then blames them for his own
mistakes"*); Ash Wednesday as *"lots of crazies with black ash on their
foreheads"* (9 March); a link to **outcampaign.org**, Richard Dawkins's Out
Campaign, on 20 March; the Catholic Church's abuse defence read as *"danger in
a culture of sexual repression"* (19 May); *"Seems pathetic that we can't deal
with tragedy without evoking 'God'"* (25 August); and on 16 December, *"RIP
Hitch."*

The hardest one is **11 November**, on Joe Paterno and the Sandusky case:

> *"Joe Paterno says 'pray for the victims', I say that's not enough. You don't
> think those kids were praying for the rape to stop?"*
> *"And where was God? Presumably watching from above. Arms folded. Doing
> nothing."*

That is the problem of evil, stated in the vernacular, about an atrocity
seventy miles from where he grew up, four days after it broke.

## The 2011 politics are anti-authority, not yet left — and that is the finding

`wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn` dates the democratic-socialist identity to
August 2020, and `wiki/self/twitter/2009` establishes that the Republican
identity was gone by November 2009. 2011 fills the middle, and what it shows is
**not** an eleven-year drift leftward. It shows a fully-formed suspicion of
vertical authority with no economic content whatsoever.

The year's political posts:

| Date | Target | Register |
|---|---|---|
| 2011-02-26 | Glenn Beck — *"DANGEROUS"* | media demagoguery |
| 2011-03-07 | Iraq war budget vs. science funding — *"they could fund the manhattan project 20 times"* | state priorities |
| 2011-03-31 | *"money=debt. debt=money"* | monetary system |
| 2011-05-26 | *"WHY DO WE NEED MOUNTED POLICE. ITS 2011"* | policing |
| 2011-08-19 | West Memphis 3 released after 17 years | wrongful conviction |
| 2011-09-21/22 | Troy Davis execution, three posts | capital punishment |
| 2011-12-02 | *"1984 = 2012"* | surveillance |
| 2011-12-15 | *"fuck off chris dodd"* | SOPA / internet regulation |

**Every item is a vertical: a church, a broadcaster, a police force, a court, an
executioner, a monetary authority, a censor.** Not one is redistributive. There
is nothing about wages, labour, healthcare, housing or class — and *"money=debt.
debt=money"* is, if anything, the vocabulary of the 2011 libertarian internet
rather than the left one. In 2011 this profile could have gone either way.

The Troy Davis posts are the clearest window on the method, because he refuses
the easy version of his own position:

> *"I have no idea whether Troy Davis was guilty or not, but he deserved the
> chance to live another day to fight in court."*

He argues the procedural case rather than the innocence case, which is the
harder and less satisfying one. This is `wiki/mind/synthesis/vertical-authority-skepticism`
at twenty-two: the objection is to the authority's claim to be certain, not to
the outcome.

**So the refinement to the left-turn account is this.** The disposition was
complete by 2011 and the *content* was not. What August 2020 supplied was not
an anti-authority instinct — he had that at twenty-two — but an economic
analysis to attach it to, and a name for the result. That is a smaller claim
than "he was always on the left" and a bigger one than "he converted in 2020."

## The career goes backwards, in public, all year

The 2010 studio job does not survive January. The sequence:

- **3 January** — *"boss tries to follow me on twitter. no thanks."*
- **23 January** — *"the never ending job hunt."*
- **27 January** — *"new internship starts on Friday. oh boy oh boy- I missed working for FREE!!!"*
- **28 January** — *"first day at my marketing internship. I'm...uh.. marketing things?"* and, hours later, an advance LP as a perk: *"like, don't ever pay me, just more of this."*
- **21 March** — *"working internships: you spend the first half grateful for the opportunity, and the second half miserable from / not getting paid."*
- **21 October** — *"What the fuck there's 100 dogs at the office, and no I'm not employed as a dog walker."* / *"Correction: I am a dog walker."*

He is twenty-two, credentialed, in New York, and working for free. The joke on
28 January — accepting a free record in place of money and finding it a fair
trade for one afternoon — and the correction to it eight weeks later are the
same person arriving at the obvious conclusion slowly and out loud, which is
the shape `wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch` describes.

## Irene: the middle instance of a three-point sequence

**25–28 August**, Hurricane Irene. He is out of town, and every post is about
consuming it from a distance:

> *"So bummed that I won't be here for the storm, not so bummed that I will
> have power and running water."*
> *"godspeed NYC. wish i could be there to ride the storm out with you :("*
> *"glued to TV, must make sure my apartment will be standing when I return."*

He also posts the official NYC evacuation map to his followers, which is the
liveblogging reflex that shows up fully during Sandy fourteen months later.

That gives the digital-witness thread three dated points rather than one:
**Balloon Boy (2009) — the coverage as the subject; Irene (2011) — the storm
consumed remotely and redistributed; Sandy (2012) — the observation finally
stated as a claim about the medium.** The escalation is toward articulation,
and `wiki/mind/synthesis/millennial-digital-witness` now has a curve instead of
a single data point.

## The public forensic audit of a stranger, 27 February

Three tweets, at an acquaintance:

> *"@KRISTARENAE25 you should take your location off if you're going to lie
> about going to concerts, unless Lady Gaga was in Lawrencevile"*
> *"also this pic is two years old- which is painfully obvious because you
> don't have your 'signature' lip ring."*
> *"hey no hostility! i just understand the fact that while twitter loc may be
> off by a few blocks, it's never that wrong. whatevz"*

Two independent lines of evidence — a geotag and a physical detail in a
photograph — assembled into a proof, published unprompted at the person, and
then followed by *"hey no hostility!"* as if the audit had been a favour. This
is the corpus's anomaly-detection orientation deployed socially at real cost,
dated to 2011, and it is the earliest instance of it on a named living person.

## StarCraft II: from five minutes to a tournament in three months

On **20 March**: *"the maximum amount of time that i'm interested in playing
sc2 is ~5min. open game> patch download> close game. best $60 i've ever
spent."* On **23 June**: *"Playing first round in GeForce pro/am tonight. Not
enough TvZ prep :|"*

In between and after, the vocabulary is a competitor's, not a consumer's:
ladder league placement (*"went 7 for 9 in silver league ladder games last
night. reclassified to bronze - wtf"*), a named build order (*"6 rax all
in...the magic build"*), casters (`@day9tv`), pros by handle (IdrA, HuK, DRG,
TLO, Losira, MC), the Korean term *bonjwa*, and *"i laughed, i cried, i built
15 infestors."* MLG is *"better than the superbowl"* (5 June), and he plans a
weekend around it: *"a 2.5 day long date planned with my couch and
@alexisarmel."*

Two things follow. The dismissal-to-competition curve is three months, which is
the same acceleration the corpus documents in other domains and is worth
holding as an instance. And on **27 June** he tells `@shane_brannan` *"I
dedicate the little bit of gaming time I do have strictly to star2"* — a
deliberate narrowing to one game, from someone whose 2008 record is World of
Warcraft and whose 16 December post is *"6 years of (intermittent) wow
playing... And I've finally hit level cap."*

## The tobacco quit did not hold, and that matters

`wiki/self/twitter/2010` records 13 December 2010 as day one without tobacco,
via an e-cigarette. On **10 January 2011** he posts *"1 week smoke free!"* —
which dates a fresh start to roughly **3 January**, and therefore means the
December attempt lasted at most three weeks before a relapse he never
mentioned.

That is worth correcting rather than leaving as a triumphant December entry.
The pattern the two dates together support is not "he quit"; it is **quit,
relapse silently, re-quit within a month** — and the relapse is legible only
because of an arithmetic mismatch between two cheerful posts.

## Family, and one bad day in December

His sister `@veefrank` runs through the year — encouragement before something
on 28 July, a shared reaction to the Troy Davis execution on 22 September, a
joke about Aubrey Plaza on 24 November. Then, on **7 December**:

> *"@veefrank wtf just got a text from mom"*
> *"about this I hate you all"*

Nothing else is said. It is the year's only trace of the Uniontown household
reaching into Brooklyn, and it is filed here unresolved because the second half
of it — what the text said — is not in this corpus.

## Texture

The Brooklyn of it: James Murphy boarding the L at 8th (14 June), Josh Hartnett
walking a bulldog in a Charles Darwin tank top on South 5th (16 June),
*"Bedford Ave: Trust fund or gtfo"* (3 June), a Colbert Report taping
(30 November), snow from the roof, the L train imagined drowned in a rock-filled
sack, and *"1.5 years of living in NYC without getting dropped on by a pigeon.
This is my measure of success."*

The two-line obituary style, on 23 July: *"Amy Winehouse found slightly more
dead today than yesterday"*, then — the same day as Utøya — *"sorry, reserving
sympathy for the Norwegians who actually died undeservedly."* A moral hierarchy
asserted in public within hours of both events, at a cost he does not appear to
price.

And on **28 December**, without comment: *"Cursed with an insatiable urge to
spend every penny in my savings account."*

## What would change this reading

The political table above is the load-bearing claim on this page, and it is a
claim about **absence** — that nothing economic appears in 2011. This page is a
partial fetch, so that absence is weaker evidence than the presences beside it.
If a complete export returns 2011 material on wages, labour or class, the
"anti-authority but not yet left" reading narrows to "anti-authority and also".
If the internship is identifiable and turns out to have been paid, the career
arc above is a joke rather than a record. And the smoking correction depends on
*"1 week smoke free!"* meaning what it says; if it is a rounding of four weeks,
the December quit held and nothing relapsed.
