# Twitter / X — 2016

**The year the music becomes a hobby and golf becomes a subject.** SLOPPP is
not gone — there is a Periscope bedroom DJ set on New Year's Day, a Juicy J
bootleg sent to a blog in March, and one paid-sounding live booking in
February — but the account's centre of gravity has moved. What 2016 actually
documents is a man in Fayette County with a new job he finds absurd, a
girlfriend, a set of inherited instruments, and the U.S. Open in his backyard.

It is also the year that settles a dispute the wiki was carrying about its own
sources.

## The gig that was thought to be invented

On **7 February 2016**: *"Had lit times playing @ulmdub birthday party last
night at OSU"*, with a video attached and one repost.

`wiki/interests/music/overview` spent weeks holding this line as a
**contradiction against itself**: an AI-generated secondary source quoted it,
the archive did not contain it, and the page leaned toward the reading that the
analysis had fabricated the quote. A backend fetch on 2026-09-02 recovered the
tweet — id `696416822011289601`, 2016-02-07T19:34:43 UTC, text matching
exactly — and that page now records the resolution in full.

Two consequences belong here rather than there. **The ten-year music silence
that page reported has a real counter-example** — he played a live set, in
2016, at Ohio State. And **the operator export is not a census**: it missed
this row. Every claim on every page in this directory that rests on an absence
is weakened by exactly that much, and the 2014 page has been amended to say so.

## Music as something he still does, occasionally

- **1 January** — two Periscope broadcasts, the first titled *"NYE bedroom DJ set"*. The venue is a bedroom and the audience is whoever is on Periscope at midnight.
- **7 February** — the OSU set above, the only live booking in the year.
- **7 March** — a Juicy J bootleg sent directly to `@whatdirt`, the blog that had thanked him for support in 2014. Still working the same contacts, at a much lower rate.
- **5 March** — *"god save the 808"*, a callback to the *"god save the village green"* of 2011.
- **6–7 March** — *"I just found/inherited an alto sax, clarinet and mandolin. The amount of time I'm gonna spend becoming mediocre at them will be crzy"*, then *"Got a small fortune in poorly maintained instruments here."*

That inheritance post is worth keeping for the self-knowledge in it. He
forecasts, accurately and in advance, that he will put substantial time into
three instruments and arrive at mediocre — and does it in the tone of someone
describing a known personal weather pattern rather than making a resolution.
It is the diagnosis-to-behaviour gap stated by the person it describes,
cheerfully, with no suggestion that naming it changes anything.

## A job, and the register he holds it in

On **24 February**: *"I think the people at my job training are hired actors
carrying out an elaborate and gloriously stupid performance art piece."*

That is the only description of the work he gives all year. On **12 February**
he tweets `@creativelicense` — *"having trouble contacting your Mumbai
offices"* — which is the company a Gemini session elsewhere in the corpus
describes as a salaried job that turned out to be a fraudulent trap; the joke
places him still trying to reach them in early 2016.

The pattern across 2011, 2012, 2014 and now 2016 is consistent enough to name:
**employment appears on this account only as material.** Never as a fact
stated, never as a complaint, never as a plan — as a bit, delivered to
followers, about the absurdity of the situation he is inside. `wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch`
has the fact of the career; this is the posture toward it, and the posture is
stable across five years and four jobs.

## Golf arrives, and it is inherited

2016 is the first year golf is a subject rather than a joke, and it arrives all
at once: *"Nine iron"* (1 March), a WGT Golf mobile clip from Congressional
(10 June), *"sick Canadian tuxedo on the golf course"* (12 December 2015,
immediately prior), Harambe jokes about *"these prestigious golf tournaments"*
(1 September), and four days at the **2016 U.S. Open at Oakmont** (13–16 June),
attended in person and photographed — *"Oakmont rough like backyard of a
foreclosure house"*, *"Oakmont greens on some god-mode shit"*, a rain delay.

The Oakmont simile is the interesting one. He is at a major championship in
Western Pennsylvania and reaches for **foreclosure** as the comparison for deep
rough — the local landscape used to describe the elite event sitting inside it.

Golf also completes a thread the earlier pages started without knowing it was
one: *"country club status. bloated sense of self worth"* (2009, as a joke at
twenty), *"My ratchet great grandma was the country club Queen Bee in 1960"*
(2014), and now the game itself. `CONTEXT_CORE_EXPANDED` records the family as
a Republican household with an auto-parts business; the country club is a
recurring family self-image in his writing, held at an ironic distance and
never quite dropped.

## Politics: engaged enough to be sour, not engaged enough to show up

The 2020 turn is four years away and 2016 is consistent with that, in a way
worth stating precisely because it is an absence claim on a year we know is
imperfectly archived.

What is here: *"The crowd signs at DNC are weirding me the fuck out"* and
*"agreed. Disgusting that compromised values are the norm"* (both 26 July,
during the convention); a retweet-in-substance of Keith Olbermann's anti-Trump
line on 19 October; and, on 8 December, *"I don't know much about politics but
Obama is NOT a fascist."*

**What is not here is the election.** There is no post on 8 November 2016, none
in the week after, and nothing about the result at all. The DNC posts are
directed at the *convention's stagecraft* rather than at either candidate, and
the December line opens by disclaiming expertise — *"I don't know much about
politics"* — which is not a sentence the 2020 or 2024 corpus could produce.

Read against 2011's eight sharply argued political posts, this is not a
progression. It is a man who was more politically vocal at twenty-two than at
twenty-eight, and who sat out the most consequential American election of his
adult life in public silence. That is a real data point for
`wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn`: whatever 2020 was, it was not the last
step of a gradual radicalisation, because 2016 is a step backwards.

## Fayette County, confirmed again

**21 April**, to a Pittsburgh news reporter: *"@DKaplanWTAE car chase in
uniontown went through my yard. Here's the hill where he went airborne."* Also
a Periscope broadcast of a Pittsburgh snowstorm (20 January), *"FEET of snow"*,
Kennywood in July, and the Hard Rock Cafe Pittsburgh in June.

Three consecutive years — 2013, 2014, 2016 — now place him in Fayette County
inside the window `wiki/self/location-history` records as having no
address-level data.

## Texture

A black-tie event in late February with a tuxedo he objects to on engineering
grounds (*"I feel like the designers of this tuxedo set out to create the least
practical piece of formalwear known to man"*), and the next day a note about
explaining his own *"snarky-ness and 'too cool for the room' attitude"* to a
date by pointing at a man at the valet stand. Two DJ-technique critiques of
`@djhotnix` on 4 September, one of which is a numbered breakdown of the
offending method — the Aoki objection of 2014 in miniature, aimed at a local.
*"THE GENIUS IS THE ONE WHO IS MOST LIKE HIMSELF"*, attributed to Thelonious
Monk (7 May). And two self-descriptions filed a year apart in tone: *"master of
abstraction"* (15 February) and *"Problem causer, feeling feeler, nothing
accomplisher"* (8 July).

## Under the standing directive

`@Lo_weez` appears throughout the year and `@ulmdub` several times. Beyond the
7 February gig — which is a music finding and is treated as one — **none of
that material is narrated on this page**, under the Annie moratorium in
`CLAUDE.md`. The tweets stay where they are; no account is built from them.

## What would change this reading

The election silence is the load-bearing absence here, and this is the one year
in the directory **proven** to be missing at least one row. A live-scrape pass
over November 2016 is the specific thing that would settle it, and until that
runs the claim should be read as "no election post has been recovered", not "he
posted nothing". The golf-as-inherited reading is an interpretation stacked on
three posts across seven years and is offered as a thread to watch rather than
a finding. And the employment posture claim would break if a single
straightforward post about a job turns up in any year.
