# Twitter / X — 2012

**The year the ambitions close and the media diet that produces 2020 begins.**
In January Dan is *"considering a pro career playing Starcraft"*; by August he
has worked out why he cannot (*"damn you Korean SCII pros with 15+ hour days of
ladder exp"*). In March he leaves Brooklyn for the Upper East Side. By
September he is working a retail floor, writing stories about the cartoons on
the fitting-room walls. In November he applies for an unpaid internship at a
radio show.

It is also the year the corpus's most-cited tweet is posted, and this page's
job is to show what surrounded it, because the surroundings change what it
means.

## Sandy: not a remark about a storm, three days of running a wire service

`wiki/mind/synthesis/millennial-digital-witness` rests on one line from
30 October 2012. The line is real and it is quoted correctly:

> *"Sandy is like first true 'digital disaster'. Can't remember so much user
> generated content from a storm."*

What that page could not see is that **the observation was made by someone who
had spent forty-eight hours being one of the sources.** The 29th and 30th are a
continuous operation:

- *"Important Public Service Announcement! Please update your podcasts, in case of power outage."*
- *"Grand Central Station is shut down."*
- *"NYSE will be closed tomorrow due to #sandy. First weather related closure in 27 years."*
- *"The worst of #sandy novelty twitter accounts"* — media criticism, mid-storm
- *"#Sandy pics from NYC on the liveblog gogo."* / *"#sandy liveblog updated. pics/gifs/nyc news."*
- *"Manhattan is straight empty. No cabs. No stores. No people."*
- to `@OpieRadio`: the Breezy Point six-alarm fire video, then the HMS *Bounty* sinking, then the NYPD's 911 queue figures
- quoting an MTA official on flooding exceeding the worst-case projection
- *"up all night on upper east following the storm"*

He was running a liveblog, aggregating from wire and social sources,
distinguishing signal from novelty accounts, **and feeding original material to
a national radio show** — all before writing the sentence about user-generated
content. So the 2012 line is not an observer's aside. It is a **practitioner's
observation about a practice he was executing at the time**, which is a
stronger claim than the synthesis currently makes, and it is why 2012 rather
than 2009 or 2011 is where the thing gets stated: he had just done it at scale.

The atheism is in there too, mid-storm: *"Inside scoop. Sandy is a divine smite
to voice support for the Romney-Ryan ticket."*

## The prior pipeline, named — and the ideological distance flagged from the start

`wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn` documents the media pipeline that carried
Dan leftward from 2020: the dirtbag left, BreadTube, Chapo, Seder. 2012
documents **the pipeline he was on before it**, and the two are not adjacent:

| Date | Post |
|---|---|
| 2012-03-23 | *"@joerogan still planning to have Sam Harris on the podcast? Super excited for that."* |
| 2012-08-21 | *"last week's episode with Duncan is, in my opinion, the new high water mark for JRE."* |
| 2012-08-27 | *"The Duncan Trussell Family Hour killed my extended family"* |
| 2012-02-26 | on Opie & Anthony (below) |
| 2012-12-04, 2012-12-19 | praising O&A and Jim Norton by name, including on gun policy |

Joe Rogan, Sam Harris, Duncan Trussell, Opie & Anthony. That is the 2012
American male podcast mainstream, and it is the pipeline that in the following
years produces the intellectual-dark-web audience the 2020 conversion is a
departure *from*.

**And he flags the ideological distance immediately, on 26 February 2012:**

> *"I don't get down with o&a right-wing ideology, but after hearing the
> Rumsfeld 'lizard' bit, I'll be spending the rest of my day listening"*

That sentence is ten years earlier than the 2022 account already on
`wiki/interests/opie-and-anthony`, in which the fanbase following Anthony Cumia
rightward *"radicalized [us] to leftist politics"*. It establishes that the
separation of the comedy from the politics was **explicit and conscious from
the beginning**, not a retrospective tidy-up. He was never inside the ideology;
he was inside the audience.

**The complication, stated because it cuts the other way.** On 24 March he
addresses Cumia approvingly on the Trayvon Martin case — *"'You know if I had a
son, he'd look like Trayvon.' - Pres O........doubtful"* — and on 20 June sends
him a viral clip. So the disavowal above coexists with direct, friendly
engagement with the host on a racially charged story. The honest reading is
that in 2012 Dan is a participating member of that audience who has already
told himself he is only there for the comedy, which is a more interesting
position than either half alone.

## He applied to work there

On **18 November 2012**, to two O&A staffers:

> *"what's the process like to get an O&A internship? I'm a recent music
> engineering grad with pro tools certification living in NYC."*

and, the same day, *"thanks, I just sent in an app."*

This is a dated, documented job application, and three things about it matter.
It is the **only** application anywhere in the 2010–2012 record. It is for an
unpaid internship, three years after graduating and two after the Ishlab job.
And *"recent music engineering grad"* is doing considerable work: the
certification is from January 2010 and the degree from 2009 at the latest, so
he is presenting a three-year-old credential as current, to an organisation he
had spent the year telling his followers he loved.

Nothing in the corpus says what came back.

## The Starcraft ambition opens and closes inside the year

**4 January**: *"fuck this. considering a pro career playing Starcraft."*
**19 May**, on MLG: *"Oh boy, another MLG weekend. Let's all pay our hard
earned skrilla to watch the video game we all forgot about!!"*
**27 August**: *"DAMN YOU, KOREA. More specifically- damn you Korean SCII pros
with 15+ hour days of ladder exp."*

Read against 2011 — dismissal in March, a pro/am tournament by June — the pair
of years is a complete arc: **discover, escalate to competition, declare
professional intent, then locate the exact reason it is unavailable and stop.**
The reason he lands on is not talent. It is hours, and other people having more
of them. He remains a spectator all year (`@followgrubby`, `@Artosis`, GSL
subscriptions he cannot afford) and moves his actual playing to Halo 4, DayZ,
Slender and League of Legends.

The Halo 4 sequence corroborates a finding already on
`wiki/interests/video-games` from the other direction: *"just bough halo4 and
ofc brought it home to 'open tray' error"* (8 November), *"it runs a LOT. But
it's also 6 years old"*, and on the 10th *"alright homeslice. i bought a new
xbox for halo."* A six-year-old console replaced inside 48 hours for one game.

## The floor of the career

The 2011 page ends with an unpaid marketing internship and a dog-walking joke.
2012 goes lower and says less about it:

> *"If I had a peso for every South American I meet at The Gap who speaks zero
> English.........................I'd have a lot of fucking pesos"* (29 September)
> *"Sometimes I write my own stories about the cartoons on the fitting room
> walls at work."* (30 September)

Retail, with fitting rooms, and boredom deep enough to have generated a private
fiction about the wall art. In the same week: *"I'd sooner buy a GSL premium
sub than a new (probably factory scratched) iPhone. Unfortunately, neither is
in my current budget."*

Four years after *"Really feels like life has started over now"* in Winter
Park, the record shows a Pro Tools-certified audio engineer on a shop floor in
Manhattan, and it shows it in an aside about customers rather than as a
complaint. This is the material `wiki/mind/synthesis/failure-to-launch` is
about, at its lowest documented point, and the tone is the finding: he does not
mention it as a problem once all year.

## Brooklyn ends, 29 March

> *"Goodbye, Brooklyn!"* (29 March) → *"Hello, Manhattan."* (30 March)

Then the check-ins move with him — Osso Buco on the Upper East Side, Josie's
West, Le Pain Quotidien, New York Sports Club, the 9/11 Memorial — and by
October he is *"up all night on upper east."* This dates the end of the
424 Bedford Ave tenancy to the last days of March 2012 and corroborates
`wiki/people/alexis-armel`'s description of it as a two-year run, to the month.

## Politics: an Obama voter with a left-libertarian list, and no redistribution yet

2011 showed anti-authority instincts with no economic content. 2012 adds
electoral engagement and the first economic argument, and still nothing
redistributive.

The year opens disillusioned — **3 January**, *"The one thing I've taken away
from the last 3 years: no we can't."* — and ends enthusiastic. On election
night he live-tweets Obama warmly and with a caveat he repeats: *"LOVE THIS GUY
(except those pesky drone strikes)."* The next day he issues a programme:

> *"Let's do this Barry: end corporate welfare. establish congressional term
> limits. stop DEA raids of marijuana vendors. GTFO afghanistan etc"*

Corporate welfare, term limits, drug enforcement, withdrawal. Every item is
still a **limit on an authority** rather than a transfer to anybody, which is
2011's pattern continuing. The first genuine economic reasoning in the corpus
arrives the same day, in an argument with a friend: *"how do you think they
would cut taxes, increase spending, and create jobs? Simultaneously"* — a
fiscal-arithmetic objection, not a distributive one.

He is hostile to the Republican field all year (Santorum, Akin's *"rape is not
ALL bad"*, *"GOP is the Andrew Dice Clay of politics"*) and puzzled rather than
sympathetic about Ron Paul's evangelical support (*"Does not compute"*).

One line prefigures 2024 exactly. On **7 November**: *"I refuse to believe that
tonight's earlier results were as close as we were told. Need to keep it tight
for ratings."* Election-night coverage read as a **product being managed for
audience retention** — twelve years before the 2024 corpus does the same thing
at length.

## The register, which the wiki should not launder

2012 is the year the O&A vocabulary is loudest in his own writing, and it
includes repeated homophobic slurs used as punchlines — *"Faggotry abounds"* on
election night, *"IF YOU'VE EVER WORN A PINSTRIPE SUIT, YOU MAY BE A FAGGOT"*,
*"grow a beard you cunty faggot"* at an acquaintance — plus *"PSU wins this
years NAMBLA trophy"* two days after the Freeh report.

It is recorded here without softening for two reasons. It is what the audience
he was in sounded like, which is the concrete content of the phrase *"o&a
right-wing ideology"* he had already disavowed in February and was still
speaking. And it is the baseline against which the later change is measured: a
corpus that quietly omits this cannot then claim the 2020 turn as a real
movement, because it would have deleted the distance travelled.

## Texture

The Gatsby bit on **28 June** — *"The person to correctly guess how many
followers I lose when I tweet the full, unabridged 'The Great Gatsby' tonight
wins $10 amazon card"*, followed by *"Also if you think I'm kidding I will
carve you up son"* — a bit built entirely out of the platform's own mechanics.
The Chris Brown tattoo hoax (26 January) and the retraction the next day
(*"I do NOT have a Chris Brown tattoo rofl"*). *"I blogged about a world
without internet"* (28 August), after a four-hour outage described as *"dying a
slow, tortuous death"* — evidence of a blog this corpus does not otherwise
hold. A podcast proposed to `@adamlucidi` on 27 August, of which nothing else
survives. Jumping the subway gates *"Vin Diesel style"*. And a prediction filed
on 29 September: *"years from now Die Antwoord will be praised as one of the
most elaborate and unique pieces of performance art ever."*

On **23 February** he writes the sentence that is the only place in the corpus
he names his own core group unprompted:

> *"People who need to come visit me: @danpolyak @EricJester @JoshBrannan
> @Woodguts"*

Four handles, three years into New York, listed as the people who ought to be
visiting and are not.

## What would change this reading

The Sandy claim above depends on the liveblog having been his; the link is
dead and only the tweets attest it, so if the liveblog turns out to have been
someone else's that section reduces to aggregation, which is weaker but not
nothing. The retail job is inferred from two posts and no employer is named —
The Gap is where he *met* people, not necessarily where he worked. The O&A
application has no recorded outcome, and if one surfaces showing he was hired,
the failure-to-launch reading of this year changes shape entirely.
