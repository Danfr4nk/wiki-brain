# Twitter / X — 2020

**Twenty-five posts, and two of them move the corpus.** 2020 is the thinnest
year on the account since 2008, and it is the year
`wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn` is named after. What it contains is the
conversion happening in public, at two separate moments, neither of which is
the one that page dates.

## 3 October 2020: the declaration

> *"okay so if 2020 hasn't made you a marxist who is unapologetically claiming
> democratic socialism / social democracy as your political ideology...you're
> either frighteningly uninformed or just a fucking ghoul"*

That is the first public political self-identification in seventeen years of
this account, and it is **independent corroboration of the conversion the
synthesis dates to a private self-narration of 2020-08-22** — same year, six
weeks later, no AI in the loop, addressed to strangers.

Its register matters as much as its content. It does not describe a position;
it issues an **ultimatum**, and the only two categories it allows for
disagreement are ignorance and moral defect. Six weeks after adopting an
ideology he is already policing its boundary. `wiki/mind/profile/index`
describes a self-verdict with no middle setting, and this is that mechanism
pointed outward at everyone else.

## 15 and 23 February 2020: the analysis arrives six months before the label

The 2019 page establishes that in October 2019 Dan was joking about voting for
Cory Booker and addressing *"moderate conservatives"* as a fellow traveller.
Four months later:

> *"you know who loves the idea of mike running? the guy who was/is working to
> influence the election for trump. he was floating the idea to him before the
> last election to further splinter the party by diving moderates away from
> their implied front runner."*
> *"super tuesday is going to be a fucking trip. bloomberg talked openly in
> 2016 about a strategy of spend stupid amounts of money to get enough
> delegates for a brokered convention. i fear he's going to try to pick up
> buttigieg as VP, knowing boomers will see it as party 'unity' lol"*
> *"@BenjaminPDixon old bloomberg footage after losing stop and frisk court
> ruling on why body cameras were totally unnecessary and counterproductive"*

This is not liberal-resistance material. It is **primary-season left analysis**
— the billionaire as a party-splitting instrument, the brokered convention as a
purchase, stop and frisk as the disqualifying record, and the interlocutor is
Benjamin Dixon, a left commentator. On 8 February he had already posted a
*"Prioritized Budget"* joke; on 15 February he is running the Democratic
primary as a class conflict.

**So the transition sits between October 2019 and February 2020, and its
occasion is the Democratic primary rather than the lockdown.** The refinement
to `2020-left-turn` is a sequence rather than a date:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Oct 2019 | liberal — impeachment as procedural drama, a Booker joke |
| **Feb 2020** | **left analysis, fluent, in the primary** — before any lockdown |
| Aug 2020 | the label adopted, privately, in the self-narration that page quotes |
| **Oct 2020** | **the label declared in public, as an ultimatum** |

The lockdown reading that page documents is real and it is not the origin. It
is what happened to a man who had already changed his analysis and then found
himself indoors with time.

## The method, applied to the 2016 campaign

On **23 February** he opens a thread describing an archival project:

> *"i've been pouring through a ton notable press conferences, debates, town
> halls and other important moments of the 2016 election in their original
> long-form versions. They are full of sound bytes that you know well, but i
> really suggest you rewatch the full events"*
> *"There's pretty convincing evidence that the whole campaign was a visibility
> 'stunt' and the tone is markedly different than 2020 trump. Interestingly, it
> lacks the narcissistic confidence and is much closer to real populism"*
> *"I've always been curious to learn abt Dice Clay during the transition into
> the 'character' becoming the real person. Trump became TRUMP right in front
> of our eyes."*

Go back to primary sources, watch them **unedited and at full length**, compare
early to late, and report where the two diverge. That is this wiki's own method
described by its subject six years before he built the wiki — and the specific
observation, that the character consumed the man, is the one he had already
reached for in 2012 when he called the GOP *"the Andrew Dice Clay of
politics."* Eight years later he goes back and checks it against the footage.

## Texture

The year opens in New York — *"the hustle and bustle of nyc on nye"*
(1 January) — which is the only location signal after the 2019 departure from
Pennsylvania, and it is thin. Two posts on 8 February debunk a viral video
(*"spoiler alert: it's fake. as if the 'tik tok' watermark wasn't enough of
giveaway"*). Pop Smoke's death is noted on 19 February. And on **26 April**, a
sentence that is the whole of the year's emotional record:

> *"the best part of my day is eulogizing old 'ron and fez' bits with
> @sniffafart and then consequently falling into a deep deep depression about
> that era being over"*

Twenty-five posts in a pandemic year, from an account that had produced 346 in
2013. Whatever 2020 was for Dan, `wiki/mind/synthesis/2020-left-turn` reads it
from a self-narration and a YouTube history rather than from here, and this
page is the reason why: the feed had already stopped being where he lived.

## What would change this reading

The February material is four posts and a thread. Reading them as "the analysis
arrived by February" is an inference from register and vocabulary, not from a
statement — he does not say he has changed his mind, and a person can make an
anti-Bloomberg argument without being a socialist. What makes it hold is the
contrast with October 2019 rather than the content alone, and if 2019 turns out
to be missing posts, the contrast weakens. The 3 October declaration needs no
such hedging: it says what it says.
