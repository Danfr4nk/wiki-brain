# Betherin Mechling

Betherin Mechling is [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie Ulmer's]] cousin, a
member of the Mechling family whose Uniontown household appears throughout
the 2015–2016 message record as a fixed point on the Ulmer social calendar,
and the fiancée of [[wiki/people/jay-lauer|Jay Lauer]], who died of a heroin
overdose on or about April 11, 2017. She is not a person Dan has a
relationship with — she never messages him and he never messages her — but
she is structurally important to two things the corpus documents at length,
and she is the reason both of them look the way they do.

## The family position

The Mechlings sit inside Annie's extended family, and the corpus records
that adjacency casually and often: Christmas visits ("my family is going to
the Mechlings around 4"), a Memorial Day BBQ in 2016 that Dan attends and
Annie negotiates around, and a December 2015 exchange in which Dan and Annie
joke that a hypothetical hyphenate would "undoubtedly be Mechling Ulmer."
Her father is referred to only as Rusty. Dan's own assessment of the family,
written the day Jay died and therefore unlikely to be flattery, is
unqualified: "Betherin (and all the Mechlings) are the most wholesome, nice,
ethically-sound people."

Geographically the Mechling house sits inside the same small circuit as
[[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran's]] — a 2016 message asks how Dan will "get
back to Frans/Mechlings," treating the two as one errand.

## The engagement, the money, and the ring

Jay Lauer's 116-message thread with Dan is a supply thread with an unusual
feature: the person on the other end could not access his own money. Betherin
"controls all the money" (December 31, 2016), his check clearing or not
clearing is reported through her ("Betherin saying my check didn't clear"),
and his availability is described in terms of getting away from her ("I
escaped without Betherin"). That control is what produces the thread's
characteristic texture — repeated requests to be fronted, partial payments of
$10 and $20, and the reproach that Dan had said he would "look out." Read
without her, the thread is a man who is broke. Read with her, it is a man
whose fiancée had taken over his finances, which is what families do when
someone is using.

The engagement ended shortly before he died. Dan's account, given to his
father the same evening, is that "there was a domestic issue recently and she
gave him the ring back. It's a mess, they had just bought a house together."
He also records that both the Mechlings and the Ulmers had disliked the
relationship — "for reasons which are now only too obvious."

## After

[[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen Ulmer]], Annie's mother, called Dan on the
night of April 10, 2017 to tell him Jay had died. His condolence message to
her the next evening is the **first message in a thread that is still running
eight and a half years later**, and Ellen's reply names Betherin as the
person the family was most concerned for: "I appreciate the offer and will
keep it in mind as things progress — especially with B. E." Dan and Annie
attended the early viewing on April 17, 2017, where Dan reports that "Mr.
Mechling said to tell you hello"; the funeral was the following day.

Nothing in the corpus after April 2017 mentions her again.

## Gaps

Her surname's spelling is taken from the operator capture and her first name
from the message corpus, which uses "Betherin" consistently; no independent
document confirms either. The relationship to Annie is given as "cousin" by
the capture and is consistent with the corpus's "Mechling/Ulmer cousins"
framing, but the exact degree — first cousin, or a cousin through the Ulmer
side generally — is not established. Nothing records what became of her, the
house she and Jay had just bought, or whether she remained in contact with
the Ulmers. The nature of the "domestic issue" is not described anywhere and
is not inferred here.
