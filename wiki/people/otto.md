# Otto

Otto is [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]]'s nephew — the younger child of
[[wiki/people/claire-ulmer|Claire]] and [[wiki/people/garrett]], and the *"unnamed
nephew"* that Claire's page has recorded as a gap since it was written. He appears
**31 times** between October 2023 and June 2025, roughly half as often as his
sister [[wiki/people/alice]].

The record is thin and domestic, and its value is that it dates and orders the
household. His birthday falls in **late October** — on 2023-10-29 Annie reports
going to *"ottos party"* after visiting her grandmother's new apartment, with
Claire having cancelled the children's half of the guest list because *"Otto isn't
feeling well. So it's just family now."* He is collected from school (2024-02-23)
and has shoes bought for him the same afternoon.

**He is clearly the younger of the two.** In March 2025 Annie sets out the
afternoon in a single line — *"Nap time is at 1:00 until 3:00 for Otto.. while
Alice has quiet time"* (2025-03-07) — a sleep schedule against a school-age
sister's supervised rest. In July 2024, holding one child and standing next to
the other: *"She wouldn't let me put her down and Otto didn't want up lol win
win."*

By 2025 he and Alice are handled as a pair: soccer practice together
(2025-03-19), Sweet Frog (2025-05-08), a sleepover with pedicures *"from aunt
Annie"* (2025-05-17), and church the next morning at their own request. On
2025-06-16 Annie is watching both at 1:00 while Claire is at a doctor's
appointment — one of several instances where the childcare is scheduled around
Claire's needs rather than Annie's availability, which is the pattern
[[wiki/people/alice]] treats at length.

**A third child, Waylon, appears exactly twice**, both on 2024-07-11 and both
Thursday: *"Otto Alice and waylon"*, and Dan's reply, *"Waylon Jennings?"* Two
mentions in a 217,573-message corpus is not enough to establish whether Waylon is
a third sibling, a cousin or a friend, so no page is created and the fact is
recorded here and on [[wiki/people/claire-ulmer]] rather than lost.

## Gaps

1. **Surname**, as for Alice — Claire's married name is not in the corpus.
2. **Birth year.** Late-October birthday established; the year is not. The nap
   schedule in March 2025 suggests roughly 2–4 years old, which would place his
   birth around 2021–2023, but that is inference from one line.
3. **Who Waylon is.** Two mentions, one of them a joke about a country singer.
   Answerable by the operator in a word.
