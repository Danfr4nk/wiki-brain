# Lucy

Lucy was [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz's]] Jack Russell terrier — elderly
and fully blind by 2015 — and she appears in the corpus at two moments, both
of which belong to somebody else's story.

The first is [[wiki/legal/2015-possession-arrest|the 2015 possession arrest]].
Dan had taken her along on the 3am errand that ended in a traffic stop, and
when the troopers found the narcotics pack during the inventory search he was
cuffed with the dog still in his arms. The detour the police then made to the
pool hall on the way to the barracks was partly to hand her back.

The second is her death. A message in the corpus records it plainly: Suz "put
lucy down a couple days after fran died because we wanted to get all the
awful-ness out of the way at once." [[wiki/people/fran-coldren|Fran]] died on
April 4, 2018, which dates Lucy's euthanasia to within a few days of it. The
reasoning is worth recording as given — the timing was chosen to consolidate
two losses into one period rather than to follow the dog's own decline, which
is the only documented instance in the corpus of a death being scheduled
around another death for the convenience of grieving.

A third, undated message carries the only affectionate trace of her: "do you
remember when we went to cheat lake to take lucy and you were upset and saying
goodbye to her in the back seat?"

**Gaps:** her age, when she went blind, when she joined the household, and the
date of the Cheat Lake trip are all unrecorded. Whether the euthanasia
decision was medical as well as logistical is not stated anywhere — the only
account is the one-sentence explanation above.
