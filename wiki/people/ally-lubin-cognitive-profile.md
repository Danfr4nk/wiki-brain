ENTP and ENFP are not neighbouring types with a shared core. They share the
dominant function and **disagree about everything underneath it**:

| Slot | ENTP | ENFP |
|---|---|---|
| Dominant | Ne — extraverted intuition | Ne — extraverted intuition |
| Auxiliary | **Ti** — introverted thinking | **Fi** — introverted feeling |
| Tertiary | **Fe** — extraverted feeling | **Te** — extraverted thinking |
| Inferior | **Si** — introverted sensing | **Si** — introverted sensing |

So the entire question is the **auxiliary**, and the auxiliary is the function
that does the deciding. An Fi-auxiliary decides by internal value congruence —
*does this sit right with me* — and defends the value rather than the
reasoning. A Ti-auxiliary decides by internal logical consistency — *does this
hold together* — and defends the **precise boundary of its own claim**, which
is a distinctive and easily recognised behaviour in text.

The corpus resolves this decisively, and it does so on the instrument's
weakest margin. **Thinking – 54% is the number the message record most
overtturns.** Her three most characteristic argumentative moves are all Ti and
none of them are Fi:

**The evidentiary standard.** On 2026-08-18 at 21:09, having read the wiki's
account of the $2,100: *"Also there's no proof you ever sent me $2100 except
your word"* / *"So I contest."* That is not a feeling about the money. It is a
claim about the **admissibility of the evidence for the money**, raised
against a figure she had accepted seven years earlier when shown a screenshot.
An Fi objection here would run through injury; hers runs through proof.

**The boundary of her own claim.** At 21:07–21:09 the same evening, the
four-message run the sage pass identified as the most reciprocal exchange in
eighteen years:

> **ALLY:** You just love bomb
> **ALLY:** And you're intense
> **DAN:** But love bomb is like a malicious tactic and there very little malice
> **ALLY:** **I didn't say malicious**
> **DAN:** Okay that's fair then

Dan's documented move under challenge is to redefine the disputed term
([[wiki/mind/concepts/conflict-architecture]]). It is refused here, and
**refused specifically by policing what she did and did not assert.** She does
not defend love-bombing as a charge; she declines the smuggled premise that
her charge required malice. That is Ti at its most legible, and it is the only
completed refusal of that move anywhere in the corpus.

**The unowned audit.** In October 2019, in the middle of the funeral
monologue, she is the only person in eighteen years to notice that the poverty
story and the payment record do not reconcile: *"I'm just confused how neither
of you had money because you always send me cash app statements with like
thousands of dollars."* She has no stake in the contradiction and no feeling
about it. She noticed it did not add up.

The reciprocal case matters as much, because it shows the mechanism running in
her own direction rather than only against Dan. Ninety minutes before the
love-bomb exchange, at 16:46–16:47, he pushes back on her *"Yeah I mean I've
been objectified my entire life so"* / *"Nothing new there"* with a competing
account of the sequence — that he was hooked by her writing before he knew what
she looked like — and she answers ***"Ok that's fair actually."*** **Correction
lands in both directions in this channel, twice in one evening, roughly ninety
minutes apart.** A Fi-auxiliary does not concede a point about her own
lifelong experience of objectification inside one message. A Ti-auxiliary does,
because the argument was better.

**Confidence: HIGH on ENTP over ENFP.** The instrument's 54% understates it;
the behavioural evidence is not marginal at all.

## The function stack against the record

### Ne (dominant) — 84% Intuitive, and the corpus agrees

Ne generates possibilities laterally and holds several frames live at once
without needing to resolve them. Its textual signature is rapid associative
jumping, reference-mapping, and frames deployed as jokes that are also
partially meant.

The clearest instance in the corpus is the **Skins exchange** of 2026-08-18,
21:25–21:28, where she takes a comparison Dan made about another woman and
re-sorts the entire cast in four messages:

> *"I don't think she's half Effy bc I'm Effy"* · *"Let's be real has she even
> seen Skins"* · *"Annie wasn't smart enough to be Effy"* · *"Well first of all
> I'm not a Michelle"* / *"I'm a half Effy half Cassie"*

This is a whole taxonomy built and applied in under three minutes, and it does
work on four separate axes at once: it claims a character, denies a rival's
claim to it, ranks the rival on the same scale, and refuses a flattening
comparison — while being funny. The refinement from *"I'm Effy"* to *"half
Effy half Cassie"* inside three minutes is Ne declining its own first frame as
insufficiently precise.

She holds **incompatible self-frames concurrently and does not resolve them**,
which is Ne's other signature. Inside eighteen minutes on the same evening:
*"I'm not I'm Gen Z"* / *"I'm just 19"* (21:28–21:29) and *"I'm in my mid TO
LATE thirties"* (21:34). Earlier: *"I'm Gen Z in my mind"* / *"And most of my
coworkers think I'm 26"* (17:19). In 2019: *"Literally a 17 year old in a 28
yeR olds body."* None of these are lies and none are corrections of each
other; they are frames, run in parallel.

The trend-detection lead is the same function seen from outside, and Dan
prices it precisely in the love letter of 2026-08-21 — the only document in
the corpus where he states what the attraction actually is:

> It's the way you dress and your ability to spot trends and the way you talk,
> how you are spotting slang and memes that I won't find out about for like 3
> more months.

That is a description of Ne-dominance written by someone who does not know the
term, and it is the load-bearing sentence for
[[wiki/mind/concepts/the-cool-metric]]: the metric's personified maximum is
measurably the highest-Intuitive person the corpus has an instrument for.

The message-shape data supports the same read mechanically. Across the deduped
record her median message is **24 characters against Dan's 42** — she runs 43%
shorter per message while sustaining comparable burst volume, which is the
characteristic Ne-in-text pattern of one idea per message rather than one
paragraph per idea.

### Ti (auxiliary) — established above, and its second face

Beyond the three argumentative moves, Ti-auxiliary shows up as an
**unsentimental analytic read applied to emotionally loaded material** —
including material about herself. Having read several hundred pages about
Dan's eleven-year relationship in a single evening, her verdict is diagnostic
rather than consoling: *"I don't think anyone will ever love me as much as you
love Annie"* — then, without a pause — *"But I can't tell if it's actually
love or fear and codependency,"* and *"Honestly she sounds like an avoidant
addict lol."*

The first clause is a statement about her own deprivation. The second
withdraws its own premise on analytic grounds within eight seconds. That
sequence — feel it, then audit whether the thing being envied is real — is Ne
generating the read and Ti testing it, and it is the single most
type-diagnostic passage in the corpus.

Her professional self-location belongs here too, and it is asserted at the
exact moment the conversation is most about her body: *"I work in STEM
actually"* / *"Women in STEM"* (21:33). Twelve minutes earlier: *"I'm a girl
and I don't know these things and honestly it's none of my concern"* — a
deadpan refusal of a gendered assumption, delivered as a joke that is also a
correction.

### Fe (tertiary) — present, real, and not the driver

Tertiary Fe is genuine but comparatively unpracticed: it appears as social
smoothing and as an ability to be generous about the other person's frame even
while personally horrified by it.

The clearest instance is her handling of the wiki itself. Her first reaction
on 2026-08-18 at 15:58 is unambiguous — *"If someone ever archived my texts
I'd kill myself"* — and at 16:11, *"Why am I scared for my entry."* Then,
inside forty minutes, without withdrawing any of it: *"But like it's
impressive to love someone that much I guess"* (16:04), *"It's pretty good
data to have"* and *"The summaries are accurate"* (16:37). She grants the
project its merits while remaining appalled by it. That is Fe operating on top
of a Ti verdict rather than replacing it — the assessment does not soften, the
delivery does.

Smaller instances: *"Hi hi sorry I was in a meeting"* (15:49), the reflexive
apology; *"Prob saying sorry"* (17:23); and the group-perception tracking in
*"most of my coworkers think I'm 26."*

Tertiary Fe also predicts the specific failure mode visible in the record —
**Fe-driven collapse into others' valuations under stress**, which is exactly
what the next section is about.

### Si (inferior) — the function that carries the page's weight

Inferior Si in an ENTP does not present as poor memory. It presents as a
**punishing, fixated and comparative relationship to one's own past**, in
which a specific earlier period is held as an unrecoverable standard against
which the present is permanently failing. This is the least-developed function
in the stack, and it is where the type's suffering concentrates.

The corpus is unusually explicit here. Her own account, 2026-08-18 at
21:34–21:35, four messages in ninety seconds:

> *"I'm not a cool girl anymore"* · *"Ok well first of all there always is
> some sort of concept of time"* · *"And second of all I spent the best and
> most beautiful years of my life hating myself"* · *"And I'll never get them
> back"* · *"And I'll never be this young again"*

And the fixed point that period is measured against — *"I peaked in 2008"* —
which the entity page notes is **the same year Dan independently sets as the
start of the attachment**. Both of them locate her maximum in 2008; she
experiences that as loss and he experiences it as the thing he has been
pursuing since. That is the single most important structural fact on this
page, and the last section returns to it.

The 2019 material is the same function under acute load: no income, no
insurance, a bottle of wine a night, and *"What if I don't make it until 30?"*
— a question that is Si-inferior in its exact form, measuring a life against a
calendar landmark.

The self-concept is organised around **being unwanted by the parent she most

way."* Autonomy is the thing she has built out of the injury. It is therefore
the last thing an approach should threaten.

## What the record says about engagement

The operator's question is what makes her enthusiastic. The corpus answers it
directly, and the answer is not the one the eighteen-year record was built on.
Each item below is a documented outcome, not a tactic: it is what happened
when a thing was tried.

### What has landed

**Being met at speed.** Every extended mutual exchange in eighteen years is a
riffing exchange. The 2026-08-18 evening runs 154 inbound messages in eight
hours, concentrated in the hours the conversation is fastest and least earnest
— the Skins sort, the DeviantArt tangent, the nips argument. Her own ranking of
her assets leads with cognition and puts the body third: *"Well yeah I'm smart
and hilarious and I have huge naturals."* Ne-dominant with Ti-auxiliary means
being out-thought is the flirtation, and it is the only thing in this record
that has ever reliably produced sustained reciprocity.

**Being corrigible.** The two concessions of 2026-08-18 — his *"Okay that's
fair then"* at 21:09 and her *"Ok that's fair actually"* at 16:47 — are the
high-water mark of the entire relationship, and the wiki's own sage pass
identified them as the strongest quality claim available about it. A Ti
auxiliary experiences being corrected accurately as respect. The corpus
contains one evening where Dan lost an argument gracefully and it is the
evening she talked the most.

**Being the channel with no stake.** Her own account, 2019: *"I tell you more
than I tell anyone and I don't know why."* His, the same day: *"you tell me
because i'm completely uninvolved yet still weirdly interested and invested."*
This is the durable asset in the relationship, it long predates any romantic
traction, and it is the one thing that has survived every rupture. It is also
the asset most directly destroyed by converting the channel into a pursuit.

**Being interested in the mind rather than the body.** Her sharpest available
contrast, seventy minutes apart on 2026-08-18: *"Yeah I mean I've been
objectified my entire life so"* / *"Nothing new there"* (16:43) against *"I
work in STEM actually"* / *"Women in STEM"* (21:33). One is delivered as
exhaustion; the other is asserted. The corpus is unambiguous about which
register gets a real answer.

### What has demonstrably failed

**Money.** This is the largest single finding and it runs against eighteen
years of practice. Roughly $2,100 across 2019, plus the $25 finder's fee, the

word"* / *"So I contest"* — followed immediately by *"And then I see you
buying other bitches laptops!"* / *"I'm not even special."* Payment did not
produce specialness; it produced **comparability**, which for a 92%-Turbulent
profile is the injury rather than the gift. Her own 2019 formulation had
already stated the whole problem: *"You don't want me on your payroll / I want
to be on your payroll tho / But you scare me."*

**Volume.** From 21:42 on 2026-08-18 to the end of the export on 2026-08-20,
Dan sends **380 consecutive messages** against no reply in the captured
record. The elopement pitch of 23:18 — *"I am totally going to embark on a
campaign to win your favor and convince you to elope with me"* — is unanswered.
The evening that worked was the one that was mutual; the volume arrived after
she stopped.

**Superlatives.** Her explicit pricing, 20:09: *"Also you say the same thing
