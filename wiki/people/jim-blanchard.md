# James "Jim" Blanchard

James Blanchard is a real, NYC-based contact of Dan's — confirmed by a
Facebook contact card (`jtblanchard66@gmail.com`, tagged location "Nyc")
and by a single dated message, November 15, 2019: "i'm in jim blanchards
apartment," sent to a Uniontown-area contact rather than to
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] or anyone at
[[wiki/places/307-e-76th-st|307 E 76th St]]. That is the entire
independent primary record.

A 2026-08-10 operator capture names him separately, describing him as
"worlds worst building super/real estate agent" for the 76th Street
building, distinct from the landlord
[[wiki/people/john-paci|John Paci]]. Nothing in the recovered message
corpus corroborates this specific role, or the negative characterization,
or any connection to the address at all — the one dated mention predates
the operator's framing and is neutral in tone (a visit, not a
grievance). Per capture-note handling, this is recorded as testimony
about a real person, not as an independently verified fact, and the page
is filed as a stub pending stronger corroboration.
