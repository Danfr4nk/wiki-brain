He is the person [[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie]] left Dan for, the person
she says raped her on July 25, 2026, and the person whose continued presence
in her life organises every hostile hour of
[[wiki/timeline/events/july-august-2026-reentanglement|the eight days that
followed]]. He has no name in this wiki because **Dan has decided not to
learn it**, and said so twice on the record. That decision is the most
interesting thing about the page and the reason it exists.

The page is written from a single vantage: Annie's statements about him, and
one direct exchange in which he wrote to Dan from her phone. Nothing here is
independently corroborated. He should be read as a documented *presence* with
documented *effects* — the effects are heavily evidenced — rather than as a
person the corpus has established facts about.

## What he did, on the record available

**He wrote to Dan from Annie's phone.** The first appearance is a message on
her number at 00:25 on July 26: "She's a slut man lmfao." The exchange that
follows the next morning runs about twenty minutes and is the only direct
contact in the corpus. His register is short, contemptuous and aimed at
Dan's specific vulnerabilities — unemployment ("Time to get a job buddy"),
sexual humiliation ("The cuck never gives up," "Can't get anyone else"), and
Annie's escort history ("Had her fuck old men for drugs"). He then makes the
accusation that structures the following week: "I have video proof how you
fingered her for hours while she was unconscious."

**He telephoned [[wiki/people/suzanne-frank|Suz]].** This is the act with the
longest consequences. Dan's assessment — "the call to my mom was enough of a
red flag alone. Annie NOBODY would do that, it's literally crazy and noone
did anything to this dude. Noone knew he existed" — is the closest the record
comes to a characterological read, and it is the injury that Annie eventually
walked to the house on August 2 to apologise for. Dan's own account of the
asymmetry is precise: "the actual insults themselves weren't really
effective. With her, it's all I heard about for months."

**He harassed Dan for hours on July 25–26**, in an exchange Annie was present
for and, on Dan's account, endorsed in real time: "you literally told him you
were choosing him in the middle of it all happening."

**He took the transcript.** Dan published the full text log at
`caakehorn.github.io/leviathan/transcript.html` and sent it to Annie; the man
read it from her phone and forwarded it to himself. This is what caused the
publication to be retracted five minutes after Annie asked — see
[[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method|the forensic method]] — and it is the
only documented instance of the apparatus being turned against its author.

## What Annie says he did to her

Recorded as her claims. They are consistent across eight days and she never
retracts them, but the corpus holds no corroboration and this page adjudicates
nothing.

That he broke her finger, two months before July 2026, in an injury the
[[wiki/timeline/events/july-2026-recontact|re-contact]] page also records. That
he raped her on July 25 after reading her texts with Dan. That he came to her
uncle's bedroom window while she was sleeping on the night of July 25–26,
woke her, poured water over her, threw her belongings around the room, and
filmed himself in her face asking "did I rape you." That he took her phone and
ran, which is the call the police actually attended. That he later "admitted
he did what he did." That she would press charges "if I felt it was necessary
AT ANY POINT" — and that as of August 2 she had made no report and taken no
rape kit, which Dan raised with her inside the seventy-two-hour window and did
not get an answer to.

Annie's parents, on her account, know what he has done and do not want her
near him. She nonetheless spoke to him again on July 27 — the day after
promising Dan she never would — and her explanation each time is contact
initiated by him: "HE CONTACTED ME," "He came as I was sleeping / And knocked
on my bedroom window."

## The refusal to know

Twice in six days Dan states that he is deliberately not finding out who this
man is.

> "I don't even want to know their fucking name and have made a point to not
> even find out." (July 26, 18:09)

> "you do understand that the reason I won't even learn who this is, is
> because I do not want to live my life with the HATRED that I would feel
> towards one person… I want to fuck this person's life up so badly and I
> haven't because I'm not going to do something stupid and illegal to
> accomplish it." (July 28, 23:45–23:46)

This is worth stating as a finding rather than a curiosity. The operator's
defining behaviour, across every domain this wiki documents, is total
instrumentation: 181,585 archived messages, a browser history mined for
relational traces, a reconstructed discography, dashboards built from his own
text logs, and this repository. [[wiki/mind/concepts/forensic-method]] is
described elsewhere as a general disposition — the thing he does to any
question that will hold still.

Here it does not fire, and the reason given is not that the answer is
unobtainable. Uniontown is small, Annie's family knows the man, and Dan had
her phone contents published on a website he controlled. The reason is that
**the knowledge is judged to carry a cost he has decided not to pay** — a
hatred with a face, and the risk of acting on it. The method has an off
switch, it is under deliberate control, and its criterion is emotional
consequence rather than difficulty or relevance.

That is a real limit on how the corpus should describe him. Statements like
"Dan documents everything" and "the forensic engine runs on whatever is put
in front of it" are now known to be false in at least one case, and the
exception was chosen, announced, and held for at least eight days under
extreme provocation.

It also has a practical consequence for this repository: the most
consequential live antagonist in the corpus is likely to stay unnamed, because
the person who would ordinarily identify him is the person who has decided not
to.
