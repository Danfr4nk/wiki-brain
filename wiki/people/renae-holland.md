# Renae Holland

Renae Holland is a former [[wiki/work/creative-license|Creative License]]
contact — her exact role at the company is not stated in the recovered
correspondence — who is the target of a December 2011 LinkedIn
recommendation-removal request that [[wiki/people/kevin-mckiernan|Kevin
McKiernan]] sent using Dan's own email/LinkedIn account, without Dan's
full agreement. In May 2012, months after Dan left the company, he wrote
to her unprompted to explain: "I (my account, rather) was acting as a
channel for Kevin to act out his most immature fantasies and I'm glad I
can say that I didn't even help write those emails." The same email
disclosed the more serious matter — that McKiernan had Dan send altered
employment contracts to two former interns, with a clause added
prohibiting recommendations without CEO approval.

Renae's reply is the whistleblower disclosure's only independent
corroboration in the corpus: "I was pretty sure that is what was going on
over there... Trust me, in my time there I was Kevin falsify more
documents that I can count — I know what he is capable of." She also
references "his attack against me" — implying she was herself a target of
some McKiernan campaign, distinct from the recommendation-removal incident
— and asks to speak by phone to "get a little more insight" into what
happened after she left. A follow-up call was arranged (May 3–4, 2012);
its content, if the call occurred, is not in the recovered record. An
earlier, undated December 2011 exchange (a forwarded "***"/"THEY SEE ME
TROLLIN'" joke, passed from Renae to Dan to Dan's father) shows the two
were already on friendly terms before the recommendation-removal incident.

**Gaps:** what "his attack against me" refers to specifically is
unrecovered — a real, chaseable lead if this thread is followed up.
Renae's exact job title or dates at Creative License are unstated. Whether
the arranged phone call happened, and what it covered, is unknown.
