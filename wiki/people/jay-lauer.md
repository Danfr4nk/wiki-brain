# Jay Lauer

Jay Lauer was a friend of Dan's from the Uniontown-area circle around
[[wiki/people/annie-ulmer|Annie's]] family — engaged to Annie's cousin
[[wiki/people/betherin-mechling|Betherin Mechling]] — and a mutual
suboxone-and-cocaine trading partner across a seven-week stretch spanning the
2016–2017 New Year. **He died of a heroin overdose on or about April 11,
2017**, two months after the thread's last message. He is the only person in
the corpus whose death is attributable to the drug economy Dan was inside at
the same time, and the record of the day he died is one of the most
revealing single days in the whole message archive.

## The thread (December 23, 2016 – February 11, 2017)

The 116-message thread (`+17248121368`) has real friendship texture rather
than the mechanical register of the dealer threads elsewhere in
[[wiki/mind/synthesis/supply-network]]: the two visit each other's houses,
plan to bring an acoustic guitar over, share a friend group, and reference a
New Year's Eve gathering at "Claire's." What organizes it, though, is money
Jay does not control. Betherin held his finances, and his side of the
conversation is a chain of workarounds — "Betherin controls all the money,"
"Betherin saying my check didn't clear," "I escaped without Betherin hmu."
The requests are small and constant: $10, $20, $30–40, promises to settle
when a check clears, and a reproach when Dan doesn't come through — "I need
some bad man.. but I blew all my money under the impression you would look
out."

The register swings violently. A January 19, 2017 exchange about sunglasses
escalates into an unprompted and offensive September 11 joke, then reverses
into an apology within minutes ("Sorry I got carried away"). Brief anti-Trump
and impeachment commentary appears in late January. The thread stops on
February 11, 2017 with an unanswered "Yo."

## The death, and how it is dated

The operator capture that prompted this rewrite dates the death to "early
2017" and asks for the exact date to be researched. The corpus supplies it
with unusual precision, because Dan spent April 11, 2017 telling four
different people at four different levels of candour.

| Time (2017-04-11) | To | What |
|---|---|---|
| 11:07 | Annie | "I'm literally in disbelief over jay.. i dont know what to even think" |
| 14:44 | [[wiki/people/lindsay-carolla|Lindsay]] | "Did u hear about jay?" |
| 18:07 | [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick]] (father) | "Had another friend die of an overdose today" |
| 19:09 | [[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen Ulmer]] | The condolence — first message of that thread |
| 20:48 | Annie | "I had to work all day, still tying to process jay" |

The date is therefore fixed to **April 10 or 11, 2017**. Dan says the death
happened "today" on the 11th but also that "Ellen called me last night to
tell me," which puts the news in his hands on the evening of the 10th; the
discrepancy is ordinary looseness and is not resolved here. Dan and Annie
attended the early viewing on **April 17** ("Mr. Mechling said to tell you
hello. I had a cancel today so we came to the early viewing"); the funeral
was **April 18**.

## What Dan says about it

The messages to his father are the substantive ones, and they contain the
single most important fact on this page — that Dan had tried to intervene,
using his own experience as the template:

> "I tried to help him. I talked to him about Suboxone, and told him how it
> helped me finally get out of that world. He went to the doctor and sold his
> prescription for heroin. At that point, there's nothing anyone else can do."

That reframes the thread above entirely. The suboxone moving between the two
of them was not simply trade; on Dan's account it was a harm-reduction route
he had walked himself and was trying to hand over, and it was converted back
into the drug it was meant to replace. This is the corpus's only documented
instance of Dan attempting to move someone else out of the supply economy,
and it failed.

Two other things surface the same night. He describes the pattern as
familiar — "as someone who tried to help, and has gone through this a lot,"
and "I talked to her for almost an hour about how many times I've experienced
this" — which asserts an overdose history broader than anything the wiki has
so far documented. And he answers Rick by recalling Rick's own warning to
him, delivered at Cross Creek: that "there is no turning back from opiates
and heroin, that you lose a part of yourself and your ability to be happy."
The son quotes the father's addiction warning back to him on the day it is
vindicated by somebody else's death.

His own summary of the day, sent to Annie at 20:48 that night, ends on
something else entirely: work, then the family dog Sabrina being put down
that evening, then a rejection — "and got humiliated getting rejected again
because i can't even get a handjob to cheer me up… I fucking hate my life."
The next evening he wrote her the least guarded thing in that year of the
thread: "all this stuff about jay just reminds me how important it is to
protect us and you and make sure you know how much you've changed my life for
the better."

## What the death opened

The condolence Dan sent to [[wiki/people/ellen-ulmer|Ellen Ulmer]] at 19:09
that evening is the **first message in a thread that was still running in
November 2025** — Annie's mother sending him birthday wishes eight and a half
years later, through every rupture in the relationship that connected them.
The tie that outlasted the Annie relationship itself was opened by a friend's
overdose, and Ellen's reply that night makes clear what she needed from him:
"I appreciate the offer and will keep it in mind as things progress —
especially with B. E."

## Gaps

The manner and place of death are recorded only as "an overdose" of heroin;
no obituary, coroner's finding, or news item has been sought, and Fayette or
Somerset County records would settle both the exact date and whether fentanyl
was involved — Dan notes Jay was "from near Somerset." Nothing establishes
whether the suboxone Dan supplied in December–February is connected to the
prescription Jay later sold, or whether those are separate acquisitions. The
broader claim that Dan has "gone through this a lot" is unquantified: no
other overdose death is documented anywhere in the wiki, so either the corpus
is missing them or the phrase is doing emotional rather than literal work.
Zack Lauer appears as a separate Facebook contact and his relation to Jay is
unestablished.
