# Lisa Frank

Lisa is [[wiki/people/rick-frank|Rick Frank]]'s partner, present continuously
in the primary message record since **December 27, 2015** — a decade of
documented presence that had never been written into the wiki until this
pass, discovered only because the correspondence-review pass that found her
also found the much larger correction to Rick's own page (see that page's
"corrected" section). Surname is a working assumption from household usage
in the corpus, not a confirmed legal name; whether she and Rick are legally
married is unconfirmed (see Gaps).

**Occupation:** a teacher — "Lisa was thinking 5–6" (scheduling around her
work), "Lisa is really under the weather," and, during the March 2020 COVID
lockdown, "Lisa is teaching online" while Rick did yard and house work.

**Household:** consistently invited alongside Rick to family and social
events across the record ("do u and Lisa have any interest in going,"
2018 and again 2024), and living with someone named **Syd** — host of a
June 2018 birthday cookout ("ribs corn & sides") and still present as of an
October 2024 message ("Were Lisa and Syd with us?"). Syd's relationship to
Lisa (daughter, most likely, given the birthday-party framing) and to Rick
is not stated anywhere in the corpus.

**Possible wedding, September 2023.** A message from Rick — "I never heard
back from you so not sure what your plans are for next weekend. Other than
the wedding & reception will I see you?" — raises the possibility that this
is Rick and Lisa's own wedding, eight years into the relationship, but the
corpus never states this plainly; it could equally be someone else's
wedding that weekend. Flagged, not asserted.

**Gaps:** last name unconfirmed; marital status to Rick unconfirmed; Syd's
identity and relationship to the household unconfirmed; no direct message
from Lisa herself appears anywhere in the corpus — everything here is
mediated through Rick's messages about her. No independent contact record
(Facebook, Google Contacts) has been checked for her yet.
