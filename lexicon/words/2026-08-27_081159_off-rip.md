## Reading

Standard forms are **off rip** and **off the rip** — African-American English, meaning *immediately, from the outset, without preamble*. Cognate with "off the bat", but carrying an abruptness that the "bat" version does not: "off rip" implies no run-up was offered, not merely that none was taken. Dan's gloss ("at the beignning of something" — typo his) is the sense, missing the abruptness.
