# `claims/` — the claim validity ledger

**What was true, when it stopped being true, and what the wiki still asserts anyway.**

`events.jsonl` is an append-only log and the source of truth. `claims.json` is a projection regenerated from it and safe to delete. `SUMMARY.md` is the analysis surface. The public face is `wiki/meta/claim-validity.md`, generated by `bin/wiki-claims page` and served on the portal like any other page.

⚠ **This is TRACKED, in a public repository**, on the same terms as `intake/` and `testimony/`. Git history cannot be un-published. To reverse: make the repo private FIRST, verify it, and only then decide whether ignoring the files is still wanted — in that order, always.

## The hole this fills

Every date field in this repository is about the *page*, not the *claim*:

| **Field** | **What it says** |
| --- | --- |
| `date_created`, `date_modified` | when the **file** moved |
| `date_range_start`, `date_range_end` | what span of **evidence** the page covers |

None of them says **when an assertion stopped being true**. So nothing could notice when one did — and `CLAUDE.md`'s whole case for a second brain over a RAG is that "today's synthesized conclusion is tomorrow's premise." A premise that quietly expired keeps reading as a present-tense fact, keeps getting cited, and every conclusion stacked on it inherits the rot.

`bin/wiki-climb check` fires when a premise **file** is edited. It cannot fire when a premise **lapses**, because until this ledger existed there was nothing to fire on.

## Three ways a claim stops being usable

|  | **What it means** | **Where it lives** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Retracted** | It was never true | `RETRACTED.md`, gated by `bin/wiki-lint` |
| **Corrected** | It was wrong when written | Correction blockquotes, STYLE_GUIDE rule 9 |
| **Expired** | It was true, and then it stopped | **Here** |

The third is not a weaker version of the first two. *Dan lives in Morgantown* was **correct** in 2019. Retracting it says the wiki got it wrong; leaving it says he lives there now. Both are false, and the true statement — it held, then it ended — had nowhere to live.

## `ended` is not `lapsed`, and that is the whole instrument

A window closes two ways, and they mean opposite things:

- **`ended`** — the state stopped, and something *dates* the stopping. He moved out on a known day; the release exists; the job has a last day.

- **`lapsed`** — the **record** stopped. The last evidence is 2016 and there is nothing after it. The state may have ended that year, or be running today with nobody writing it down.

`bin/wiki-traits` already refuses to read an instrument's silence as falsification. This is the same refusal one layer down: **a corpus going quiet is a fact about the corpus.** Nineteen tweets to a friend that stop in 2016 do not document a friendship ending in 2016 — they document a tweet archive ending in 2016.

So `lapsed` carries `last_seen` and **never** `valid_to`, it is reported in its own band, and `bin/wiki-claims check` fails on a lapsed claim that has acquired a `valid_to`. `asof` reports such a claim as **unsettled**, never as false.

**c004 is the worked case.** MOGZART lapsed in March 2016 and the page carried it as an "archive/revival candidate" for a decade. On 4 March 2026 he posted a jump-up DnB remix under the same name. A ledger that had recorded the 2016 silence as an ending would have been *wrong about the world*; recording it as a lapse left the question open, which is what the evidence actually supported.

## Never extend a window to clear a scan hit

`bin/wiki-claims scan` reports pages marked `status: active` whose evidence stopped years ago. The tempting fix — push the window forward, mark it current, move on — manufactures evidence, and it is rule 3 of CLAUDE.md's "four things that matter most" wearing a different hat.

If the record stopped in 2016 the claim lapsed in 2016, whatever the status field says. Re-read what the evidence supports, record `lapsed` with the real `last_seen`, and let the page say the present state is unknown. **An honest ****`unknown`**** is worth more than a confident ****`active`**** nothing checked.**

## What this deliberately is not

Not a claim-level graph over every sentence. This wiki writes prose, and forcing each assertion into a record would wreck `STYLE_GUIDE.md` and produce a ledger nobody maintains. The unit is a claim somebody **chose** to put a clock on: sparse, opt-in, load-bearing.

A claim absent from the ledger is **not thereby timeless** — it is unexamined. Reading the counts as coverage of the corpus is a category error, and the public page says so in a section that cannot be dropped.

`scan` computes candidates from frontmatter the corpus already maintains, so the ledger is fed by the corpus rather than by somebody remembering — the lesson `lexicon/words/` taught by sitting unread with one file in it.

**`scan`**** is deliberately not a prose grep.** The obvious approach — search for "no longer" and friends — was measured against this corpus and is roughly all false positives: 121 hits, overwhelmingly quoted tweets in which somebody else is no longer something. `bin/wiki-crosslink` learned the same lesson about single-token name matches.

## Commands

bin/wiki-claims                    # the standing state

bin/wiki-claims scan               # structural candidates, computed from the corpus

bin/wiki-claims asof 2019-06-01    # what the record held true on a given day

bin/wiki-claims live wiki/people/shane-brannan.md

bin/wiki-claims record "..." --kind residence --valid-from 2019-01-01 --pages ...

bin/wiki-claims expire c003 --closure lapsed --last-seen 2016-03-17 --because "..."

bin/wiki-claims expire c001 --closure ended  --on 2015-12-26 --because "..."

bin/wiki-claims supersede c001 --claim "..." --on 2015-12-26 --because "..."

bin/wiki-claims vocab              # every kind, closure and evidence tier

bin/wiki-claims page               # regenerate projection, summary and wiki page

bin/wiki-claims check              # the gate (runs inside bin/wiki-check)

An expiry is **never edited** — `expire --revise --reason` supersedes it and the log keeps both. A validity record that can be quietly rewritten is worth less than none.
