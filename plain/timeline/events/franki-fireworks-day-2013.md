# The Franki Fireworks Day (early July 2013)

**The short version.** On an afternoon in early July 2013, Dan Frank took one of his mother's cars, drove to a house in Uniontown, and picked up a girl he had known since junior high. Within ten minutes, his partner of six years sent a text that detonated the relationship. Rather than turn around, Dan withdrew $350 from his mother's ATM card, bought cocaine from the family's supplier, and stayed out for more than ten hours. He describes the day as "one of the more pivotal days of my young adult life." The record supports that assessment. The six-year relationship ended. A pattern he would later name and re-run was established. And the music production identity that would define his next chapter began in the same season.

## The sequence of events

The day is documented from a single written account produced in 2026 — thirteen years after the fact. It is memory, not a contemporaneous record. The parts of it that other sources can verify are flagged below.

| **Time** | **What happened** |

| Afternoon | Dan picks up Franki Faris at 15 Holly Lane, Uniontown — her parents' address, not where she lived |
| ~10 minutes into the drive | Alexis's text arrives: "I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki." |

## The fireworks date the day, almost

The page for Franki has carried a standing note that the "July 4" framing used elsewhere in the archive had no source anywhere and was flagged rather than adopted. This account is the first source for it. It does not name the date — but a municipal fireworks show at the mall, visible from the Route 119 bypass, in early July, is a July-4-adjacent event in practical terms. The framing is now supported but still not explicitly dated, which is a real improvement on unsourced and short of settled.

## What the day detonated

**The confrontation is now verbatim.** The Franki Faris page had recorded that the wording and date of Alexis's challenge "hasn't been located verbatim, only referenced retrospectively." It has been located: "I saw your messages. I know you're with Franki." The mechanism is also newly specific — she was reading his messages, prompted by something in his behavior, which makes the discovery an act of detection rather than a coincidence.

**Alexis was displaced to a property the archive did not know was in play.** She moved to "suz's other place at 155 virginia avenue," stayed five days, and then returned to her parents in Carmichaels. The page for 155 Virginia Avenue dates its own story from a January 2015 lease. This puts the address under Dan's mother's control eighteen months earlier, and already performing the function the later page calls lair-continuity: housing a partner who has been displaced from the primary residence. The lair predates the tenancy. The second 2026 account also carries her onward: from Virginia Avenue to her mother's house, then a new boyfriend, a brief move to New York City, and a reconciliation with Dan in October 2013 — which is why this entry's span now runs to the end of October rather than stopping in July.

**Dan's mother then engineered the aftermath.** Per the account, she thought it a good idea to have Alexis and Danielle Onesi — Dan's first girlfriend and Alexis's "former enemy" — spend time together and bond over their shared grievance, "and this caused a huge thing." That is the maternal switchboard operating on the relational graph rather than the financial one, and it is the earliest documented instance of it.

## How long the break actually lasted

Until now this page could not say how long they were apart. Dan's public posts settle it.

On **August 9, 2013** he wrote *"I guess I tweet more when I'm single?"* From **August 21 to 31** he posted repeatedly with a woman named Katie Fletcher — a lookout point, a trip to the mountains, the Oddball Comedy Festival in Pittsburgh. On **September 7** he posted *"My better 1/2"* and *"Mystery girl and everything's okay now."* On **September 8**, Alexis is named again. By September 25 he is posting about her the way he always did.

So they were apart for roughly **six to eight weeks**, from July to the first week of September — and someone else was in that gap, publicly, in photographs.

That makes this the only stretch in six years when the relationship was actually off. Two things about it matter. He filled the gap almost at once: Katie appears within two weeks of him saying he was single. And when it ended, he did not announce a decision — he announced relief. *"Everything's okay now."* Nothing was said about anything being fixed.

We cannot tell whether the "mystery girl" on September 7 is Katie or Alexis. Both fit, and the next day he names Alexis.

## The other two things that started that summer

**The production identity has a date now.** The account records that the DJ controller Dan had acquired while working at ishlab and "never really touched" was picked up after the return from New York, and that "by summer, I was dedicating my time to that — it is right as this began to take hold that I remember this all taking place." The music overview page has carried the origin as "operating since approximately 2013." This is the first source that dates it inside the year and attaches it to a cause: an idle depressive interval after a failed New York attempt, with a piece of equipment already in the room.

**The Boston manhunt is the chaos preference's missing exhibit.** Describing the months between the return and this day, Dan records almost nothing except "a few days of no sleep as i watched the boston bombing and subsequent manhunt unfold" — and, separately, a disappointing binge of *Arrested Development*. The chaos preference page names as its most serious gap that "the Boston-bombing coverage response used to frame the request is not attested in the searched corpus." It is attested now. The detail that carries it is not that he watched, but that he did not sleep for days doing so, during a period he otherwise describes as apathetic depression. The entropy appetite was the one thing running.

## The taste marker, and what it did

Franki put one song on him that day: "17" by Youth Lagoon. His own account of its effect is the clearest statement of the cool metric in the archive applied to a person in real time — "FUCKING NOBODY IN UNIONTOWN WAS LISTENING TO YOUTH LAGOON" — and the consequence he reports is total: it moved her from a list of ordinary attributes he names flatly ("brunette, skinny af, drug-accepting, daddy issues") to somebody he "immediately bowed down before to worship."

Two things are worth separating here. The attribute list is a type, stated by Dan as routine and recurring. The song is a credential, and it is what converted the type into an obsession inside a single afternoon. Under the cool metric, familiarity with an obscure record is not a preference but evidence about how a person came to know things — sought, not received — and this is the sharpest documented case of that judgment being made about a person and acted on immediately.

## What would prove this reading wrong

1. **A dated ATM withdrawal on Suz's account.** This would fix the date and confirm the $350 figure.

1. **A Uniontown fireworks schedule for 2013.** This would confirm the July-4-adjacent framing.

1. **A contemporaneous text or message from the day.** The whole page rests on one 2026 account. Any 2013 material would strengthen it considerably.

1. **A second source for the hitchhikers, the ropes course job, or 15 Holly Lane.** These are single-source details with no independent confirmation anywhere in the archive.

## Predictions

1. **The date will remain "early July 2013."** Without a dated bank record or fireworks schedule, the day will stay approximate.

1. **~~The five-day figure will stay attached to Alexis's stay, not Franki's.~~** **This one has now come true, and sooner than expected.** A second account, given independently in August 2026, confirmed the five days unprompted: "She spent 5 days at Virginia ave and then went to her moms." The five days were Alexis's, never Franki's.

1. **The day will continue to function as a pivot point.** It is too densely consequential — the relationship collapse, the production identity, the maternal switchboard, the chaos preference — to lose its status.

## What we still don't know

- **The exact date.** "Early July 2013" plus a fireworks display is as close as the record gets.

- **~~The five-day figure is now attached to two different things.~~** **Settled in August 2026.** A second, independent account confirmed the five days belong to Alexis's stay at 155 Virginia Ave, not to the length of the Franki relationship. Franki's own tenure is described only as an immediate collapse.

- **Nothing here is contemporaneous.** The July 9–31, 2013 self-typology emails and the August 14, 2013 post-mortem already in the archive are the nearest dated material and they corroborate the window, not the day.

- **The hitchhikers, the ropes course job, and 15 Holly Lane** are single-source details with no independent confirmation.
