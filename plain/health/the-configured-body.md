# The Configured Body

**The short version.** Dan has exactly two modes for his body, and no third. He **specifies the inputs** — with an absolutism that covers which vegetables may touch a sandwich and which milligram of which molecule holds the floor. He **monitors the outputs** — continuously, painfully, for how the result reads to other people. What is missing from a seventeen-year record is the mode in between: **maintenance.** Nothing in this corpus is kept in repair. The body is not neglected — it is one of the most intensively managed objects in the whole archive. It is managed the way an instrument is managed: configured at the input, read at the output, and never serviced.

## The rule, stated so it can be broken

Dan operates his body through two faculties and only two. **Specification** governs what enters it and is enforced preemptively, absolutely, and without negotiation. **Surveillance** governs how it presents and runs continuously at high cost. There is no maintenance faculty: no preventive care, no repair regime, no recovery protocol, no baseline measurement anywhere in the record. Damage is therefore not prevented and not repaired — it is absorbed, and then re-enters the system as a new surveillance object.

## Mode one: specification

The specification faculty is the best-documented thing in the health domain and it is uniform across substances and food, which is the finding — the record has been treating them as two subjects.

| **Domain** | **The specification** | **Enforcement** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opioid floor | Suboxone daily since February 2010, 1–2 mg, sixteen years, zero relapses | Absolute; the one regime never interrupted |
| Stimulant layer | Cocaine, framed as "CPU & RAM," dosed to the ceiling money allows | Continuous |
| Alcohol | **Zero**, thirteen to fifteen years, active aversion | Categorical exclusion, no exceptions in the record |
| Heroin | Tried once, winter 2008, refused — *"felt dirty"* | Refused on **aesthetic** grounds, not medical ones |
| Composition of a meal | Cheeseburger = meat, cheese, bread. Corn and lettuce permitted; no other vegetable | *"An absolute, dealbreaking, no-questions-asked policy"* |
| Onion, tomato, pickle | Refused outright — the three that permeate and cannot be removed after the fact | Refused before the plate arrives |

Read the alcohol row against the heroin row and the faculty shows its hand. Neither exclusion is a health judgement. Alcohol is excluded because it is the substance coded to the family's mechanism of betrayal — four functional alcoholics across three generations support that. Heroin was refused because of how it *felt to take*. A body being kept healthy would not produce that pair; a body being **composed** would produce exactly it.

The food-and-diet page reached the same structure independently and in a different vocabulary. Its correction is the hinge: the earlier reading held that food was simply never evaluated, and what replaced it was that food is not *ranked* but is *specified*, totally, with the refusal list consisting precisely of the three items that cannot be picked off afterwards. That is the same operation as a fifteen-year single-molecule floor with an absolute exclusion bolted to it. **Evaluation and specification are different faculties, and this body only has the second one.**

## Mode two: surveillance

The monitoring faculty is equally well documented and is aimed at a different target: not what the body contains but **how it reads from outside.** Dan's own statement of it: *"the anxious, internally probing awareness of my appearance in all senses."* The mechanism is a loop where the monitoring apparatus generates the problem it watches for. Self-Consciousness sits at the **91st percentile** on the personality instruments. The 2025 tooth disclosure is scored not as a health event but as an image event: *"the additional self-conscious, humiliating image issue."* The one food image on his body is a Misfits skull, and the design reasoning is entirely about which version of the band's logo it is.

Surveillance produces information and no action. That is not a criticism; it is the structural point. A monitoring system with no maintenance arm attached to it can only escalate, because every reading it takes adds to the queue and nothing drains it.

**Dental care exists in the record, and Dan's own account of it is wrong.** His causal chain for the lost teeth ends with *"terrified of dentist (aka haven't gone)."* The message dump contradicts the parenthesis directly: a full dental episode in autumn 2017 including surgery and a kept one-week follow-up, a 2020 appointment, a 2024 appointment. **What survives, and is the better claim: the maintenance is episodic and reactive, not absent.** He goes when something has already broken, which is the same clock the audit page documents everywhere else.

Three further things the census settles:

**The prescriber exists, and the whole relationship with him is routing.** The 2025 messages answer a gap the chemical-architecture page left open: *"I had my doctor move my prescription here,"* *"my doctor said the pharmacy won't fill an out of state prescription,"* *"my doctor tried to send a prescription to walmart and they won't fill it either so I'm completely out of options now."* The regimen is prescribed. Every recorded exchange about that doctor is about moving a script between pharmacies, and none is about a dose, a taper, a review, or a body. **The one standing medical relationship in this life is a logistics relationship**, which is exactly what the two-mode rule predicts: a prescriber is an input supplier, and input supply is a faculty this system has.

**It reverses the supply architecture.** The supply-network page frames Tom as the standing supply line for Suboxone. The June 2025 sequence runs the other way: the script fails at Walmart, and on the next day Tom is the fallback. On this evidence the default path is the prescription and the dealer network is the failover, which makes the May 2026 collapse a **second-line** failure with the first line already down, not a single point of failure giving way.

**The barrier he names is money, not fear.** Every self-directed medical sentence in the corpus is modal — *"I know you really don't want me to go to a doctor but I'm telling you I should go,"* *"I should get into a doctor for that before caddie season ends,"* *"I might need to go see a doctor if my voice doesn't start coming back"* — and none of them is followed by a visit anywhere in the record. Alongside them: *"imagine being able to just go to the doctor or dentist when you need"* and *"the doctor thing is a really big thing for me... there's no reason that 50,000 people die a year because they don't have insurance."*

## The ledger, and what it shows about the missing mode

In late August 2026 Dan started keeping a written record of everything he takes — what it was, how much, and when. The first full night of it is on record, 30–31 August 2026: one 0.75 gram batch of cocaine, worked through in six doses over nine and a half hours, and three separate 0.05 gram servings of cannabis in the same window. At the end, every gram was accounted for.

That is one night, and one night proves nothing about how often nights like it happen. Four of the six cocaine doses were entered by tapping a button that fills in 0.1 grams rather than by weighing anything, so the record is less exact than it first looks. What it shows is something else. The system he built measures the inputs to a tenth of a gram, and it will not let a batch be closed out until every part of it is explained. It is the most precise instrument anyone in this archive has ever pointed at his body — and it measures only what goes in. There is no repair in it, no follow-up, and nothing that would prompt an action. A man with no regular doctor, no lab tests and no dental schedule in seventeen years built himself a scale and a logbook for the inputs.

That is this page's whole argument arriving in a new form: the body specified at the input, watched at the output, and still never serviced.

## The asymmetry that names the mechanism

Set those modal self-sentences against how he talks about other people's bodies, in the same corpus, often in the same years.

| **About himself** | **About someone else** |
| --- | --- |
| *"I'm telling you I should go"* (2017) | *"I kept telling her to make an appointment with a doctor"* (2017) |
| *"I should get into a doctor"* (2017) | *"I wish she would have just gone to see her doctor months ago"* (2017) |
| *"I might need to go see a doctor"* (2018) | *"if you are legitimately unable to not SHIT YOUR PANTS then go to a fucking doctor"* (2018) |
| *"I'm probably going to need to go to the doctor"* (2018) | *"I just ran out of the apartment to get him to a doctor asap"* (2023, the cat) |

**Every self-directed instance is conditional. Every other-directed instance is imperative, and several are urgent enough to be rude.** Same speaker, same years, same subject matter. That is the audit rule's two clocks running inside one person's health, and it forces a genuine extension of that page. The audit rule sorts *objects* by provenance — imposed objects audited on contact, chosen objects deferred until they fail. A body is not obviously either. What the evidence shows is that **the body is split down the middle by the sort rather than filed on one side of it.** The *configuration* — the stack, the exclusion list, the composition rule — is chosen, is his, and gets the treatment reserved for the imposed: preemptive, absolute, enforced in under a second without argument. The *substrate* — teeth, lungs, the thing the configuration runs on — gets the chosen-object clock, deferred until it fails and only then reconstructed. This is the first case in the record of a single object running both clocks at once, and the seam between them is exactly where the damage lands.

## The controls

**Extreme sports rules out indifference.** Roughly 2000–2006 was a total-obsession adolescence in aggressive inline skating and terrain-park skiing — four summers at Camp Woodward, trampoline training as off-season ski conditioning, an identity built on what the body could be made to do. A person who does not think about his body does not spend six years training one. The faculty that is missing is not attention and not investment; it is *maintenance*. Training is configuration aimed at a performance ceiling, and it belongs on the input side of this page with everything else.

**The pets rule out a general fatalism about bodies.** The audit page identifies the pets as the corpus's one protected class, exempt from the forensic treatment everything else receives, and the 2023 message above is the proof it extends to their physical care: *"I just ran out of the apartment to get him to a doctor asap."* Immediate, unhesitating, no modal verb. The capacity for urgent care about a body exists intact. It is simply never pointed inward.

**The 2017 dental episode is the control on the rule's own strength.** It is the reason this page claims *deferred and reactive* rather than *absent*, and it is what a falsifier looks like when it lands partially: the rule survives narrowed.

## What would prove this wrong

1. **A preventive act.** Any documented instance of Dan initiating care before symptoms — a screening, a physical, a cleaning, a baseline lab — with no damage already in evidence. One clean case falsifies the missing-mode claim outright.

1. **A dose changed for a health reason.** The cocaine dosage arc moves with the inheritance and with nothing else. A documented reduction, taper or substitution made because of a physical effect rather than a budget would show a maintenance faculty operating inside the specification one.

*One caution about that arc, found in September 2026.* The amounts on it come from Dan looking back years later. A text he sent on 4 May 2017 — "I spend well over $100 a day on it" — works out at about **one gram a day**, because in the same conversation he insists on paying no more than $100 a gram. The arc says he was using **three and a half to seven grams a day** in that same year. Both cannot be right, and nothing in the archive settles it. What this does not change is the point being made here: the 2017 text is about money too. There is still no health reason anywhere on the curve.

1. **A recorded medical relationship that is not routing.** A conversation with the prescriber about a dose, a taper, or a body — rather than about which pharmacy will fill the script — breaks the "input supplier" reading of that relationship.

1. **Surveillance producing action.** The hyperreflexivity page records the loop generating distress; if a source shows a monitoring output leading to a repair rather than to more monitoring, the two-mode structure is wrong and there is a third mode that is simply under-recorded.

## Predictions

1. **The next physical failure will be absorbed, not treated, and will convert into a surveillance object within weeks.** The teeth are the template: damage accumulates silently, becomes visible, and is then narrated as an image problem rather than a medical one.

1. **The Suboxone supply will fail again through geography, not through Tom.** The June 2025 sequence shows the actual fragility is an out-of-state prescription meeting a pharmacy that will not fill it.

1. **No health-motivated change will follow the housing resolution.** Stabilising housing frees resources for configuration (better supply, more controlled inputs) and not for maintenance, because there is no faculty for the latter to route to.

1. **Any future "health kick" in the record will be an input regime.** A diet, a stack change, a substance protocol — never a course of treatment, a physiotherapy, or a follow-up schedule.

*Where this stands as of 31 August 2026:* leaning, but not settled. The intake ledger described above is the first new regime Dan has set himself since this was written, and it is entirely about measuring inputs — there is no treatment anywhere in it. But he built it to keep the record honest rather than to make himself healthier, so it is not squarely a "health kick," and this prediction stays open.

## What we still don't know

- **The intake ledger has one night in it.** It started recording on 30 August 2026, so it cannot say how typical that night was, and its silence before that date means only that nobody was writing anything down.

- **The census is one pass over one dump** with a fixed term list and gaps for 2022 and 2026, so nothing here speaks to the present.

- **No medical record of any kind exists in the raw data.** Everything above is inferred from what Dan said about care in text messages, which is a proxy for care and not a record of it.

- **Nothing here dates the bulimia.** Whether the adult composition regime grew out of the adolescent eating disorder or ran alongside it is the single most load-bearing unknown for this page.

- **The prescriber is unnamed and undated before 2019.** Whether the Suboxone regimen has been continuously prescribed since 2010 or has moved between prescribed and unprescribed stretches changes what the sixteen-year "zero relapses" figure is a measurement of.
