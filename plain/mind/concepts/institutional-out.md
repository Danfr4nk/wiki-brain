# The Institutional Out

**The short version.** At every job he has ever taken, Dan pre-installs an escape mechanism — a standing, unfalsifiable reason to be absent more than anyone else at the institution. His own statement of the rule, from February 2026: "i ALWAYS create an out when i get a new job. usually it's faking migraines or something similar but i ALWAYS need a way to miss more work than whatever institution i am being paid by." This is not opportunism improvised under pressure. It is a deliberate protocol deployed on hire, "before the ink is even dry."

## What the out is for, structurally

The out is the first and most reliable private objective Dan installs inside an assigned role. The fact that he installs it "before the ink is even dry" — prior to any grievance, at every job — is the strongest evidence that this is a deliberate protocol rather than something that accretes once a job turns bad. The clearest case lasted eighteen months, secured peak hours, exempted him from closing duty, and covered a simultaneous affair. None of that was the job. All of it made forty-one months of the job survivable.

The out is, in the most literal sense, a boundary-setting tool — but one that operates through deception rather than negotiation. Rather than telling an employer "I need flexibility" or "I can't work evenings," Dan invents a condition that removes the question from the table entirely. The institution cannot negotiate with a migraine. It cannot demand proof of a headache. The out converts a conversation about limits into a fait accompli.

## The migraine (default)

The preferred instrument, chosen for three engineering properties the record spells out:

- It is an *invisible pathology* — no thermometer can check it.

- It has *sudden onset* — immediate extraction from any scenario.

- It creates a boundary the institution must respect.

The fake ailment functions as a unilateral raise: hours paid versus hours actually surrendered. The institution cannot verify the claim, cannot contest it without appearing cruel, and cannot punish it without risking legal exposure. The migraine is, in game-theoretic terms, a move the institution cannot counter.

## The night class (masterpiece)

At Au Za'atar, a restaurant job held roughly 2021–23, Dan maintained a fictitious college schedule with classes "always at 9pm" for well over a year. The alibi secured peak-hour shifts and equal tip splits while exempting him from closing duty. It also doubled as cover for an affair. It survived a one-star review that asked "WAS HE HIGH?" and was never exposed.

The night class is the protocol's masterpiece because it is multi-functional: it solved a scheduling problem, secured better shifts, provided cover for a personal relationship, and did all of this simultaneously for eighteen months without detection. The fact that it was never exposed — despite a suspicious customer leaving a public review — suggests the protocol's reliance on institutional credulity is well-placed. Employers generally believe their employees, and the night class exploits that belief systematically.

## Three layers of the pattern

At the trait level, this is the refusal to be, in the record's phrase, "colonized by the clock" — a submissiveness score of 1 on the personality instruments. The out is not laziness; it is a boundary-setting mechanism so deeply embedded it operates before conscious grievance forms.

At the strategic level, it is the same game-theoretic instinct that runs through the rest of Dan's institutional dealings: engage the institution only through mechanisms it cannot verify or contest. The out is not unique to employment — it is a general posture toward any vertical claim on time.

And at the moral level, it is the standing honesty problem in miniature: the man who prosecutes off-books extraction by employers runs a standing off-books extraction of his own on the labor side. The record does not resolve the symmetry; it archives it. Dan himself supplied this concept, unprompted, to his own file.

## What would prove this wrong

1. **A job where no out was installed.** A single documented tenure where Dan had no standing alibi, no recurring absence mechanism, and no pre-planned extraction route before any grievance arose.

1. **An out deployed reactively.** Evidence that the first instance of the out appeared only after a documented conflict or deterioration at a job — which would shift the reading from "protocol" to "coping mechanism."

1. **A falsifiable alibi being contested.** An instance where an employer challenged the out and Dan could not maintain it — which would show the pattern's dependence on institutional credulity.

## What we still don't know

- The claim is that he "ALWAYS" does this, but only the Au Za'atar night class is fully documented. Whether the pattern extends to non-employment institutions — school, leases, family obligations — is unexamined.

- Whether the out is experienced by Dan as deception or as a legitimate boundary-setting tool is not in the record. The moral framing — "extraction" versus "survival" — is unresolved.

- Whether the protocol has costs Dan does not acknowledge — missed promotions, damaged relationships with employers, a reputation for unreliability — is not documented.
