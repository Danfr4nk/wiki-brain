Well, Full Sail can't be either recommended or discouraged without taking a myriad of personal factors into account... Full Sail, and more specifically, the RA program is super high intensity. You're learning a semester of knowledge in a month...for 11 consecutive month, so you have to truly love engineering and production to gain much of anything out of it. You have to excel at INDEPENDENT LEARNING, it's a huge aspect of not only FS, but the recording industry. If you don't put in hours of research and creative practice, your money will go to waste. Another thing to understand before you get involved with FS is the long road that it takes to become successful in the record industry. Even after graduating with honors, and being very involved during my training there...I'm working two jobs and an internship to try to "make it". Nobody, literally not a soul, graduates from there and is handed a job. To do this you have to eat,sleep,breathe,sweat, and bleed music. Is he serious about becoming an audio engineer?


Full Sail was great for me, I learned a lot and networked all fucking day. Made a lot of industry connections, met my fair share of celebs from it...but 99% of the people that start doing this don't make it. So if there is any doubt in your mind that this is what you want to dedicate your life to, it's probably not for you. Hope I've helped....If you're still thinking about it, I know of an amazing apartment that can be leased on the cheap.
