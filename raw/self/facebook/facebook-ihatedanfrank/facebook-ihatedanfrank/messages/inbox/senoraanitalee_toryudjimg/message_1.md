Hi Senora!


Seven years ago (the date of my last Spanish class) I embarked on a monumental quest, inspired specifically by your class. I'm proud to say that, today, I've successfully completed this adventure, and I thought I would credit you for sparking this flame of curiosity.


As I'm sure is still the case, you always played music during tests and in-class independent/translation assignments. The music provided great ambience and a clever way of exposing droves of closed-minded teenagers to the nuances of a foreign culture. One of these songs struck me as particularly beautiful, and having not heard the song for many years I still remembered the melody, cadence, and (most importantly) the lyrics from the hook. I started looking for this song when music was far less available on the internet and came up short. Every few years, something would reignite my memory of it...and I would try again.


As fate would have it, and by the most bizarre of web-surfing coincidences, I ended up seeing a video that included the song and was able to finally ID the track.
I'm not sure whether I can blame my extended search on the popularity of the phrase "te amo" or the disturbing lack of song-naming creativity on the part of Hispanic musicians, but I'm relieved to be able to close the file on this one.


Hope all is well and you're having a great summer.
