Everyone,


My album Free Trade - Plus 30 was released this week. It's a blend of hip-hop, motown, jazz, soul, you name it. Our goal with this album was to expose the brighter side of hip-hop using live instruments, insightful lyrics, and passionate musicians. I think we succeeded. For some of you who already know me, thank you for your continuing support of my music. For those who have never met me, please stay in touch and BUMP this classic summertime album in your cars. Available on www.TruStatement.com
