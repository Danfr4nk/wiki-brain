Our mom came home from the hospital over the weekend. She is extremely weak, but happy to be in her own bed. We are not so certain she will need the heart transplant. She is on eight different medications, including one to clear up an infection in her foot. Her blood pressure remains very low and must be monitored. She still cannot have calls or visitors and she has already been back to Broward General since being "released". We are told there is a long recovery road ahead. Our plan is still to get her to Jackson Memorial for an evaluation, but until Medicaid is approved this seems unlikely.


We spent some time talking with our mom about the good that has come out of this. The proof of the power of prayer and the power of community. She said she learned that "NOTHING is important except for the people in your life." We ask that you keep those prayers and positive energy coming. The fund raising helps too. We have already begun to pay for medication with the help of your donations.


We have also begun the process of forming a Non-Profit Organization in the name of Help Mend A Heart. Once our mom is better, with all of your support, we want to continue to help other people. Ordinary people who might get sick and suddenly find themselves in need. We think social media can be really powerful and we want to show people how to rally support for their own families who otherwise might not know where to begin in an emergency such as the one we were faced with three weeks ago.


We see the efforts you are putting towards this. We know everyone is doing what they can. We are grateful and we are blessed.
