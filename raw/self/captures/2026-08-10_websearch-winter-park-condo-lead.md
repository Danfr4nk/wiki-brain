**RESOLVED [2026-08-10, later same day]:** the operator supplied the exact address directly — `raw/self/captures/2026-08-10_160647_winter-park-condo-address.md`: **2924 Antique Oaks Circle, Winter Park, FL**. This is a real, verified address: unit of the **Parkview Village** condominium community (HOA registered as "Parkview Village Condo Association"), commonly listed at **350 S Semoran Blvd, Winter Park, FL 32792**, with listings describing it as directly across from Full Sail University — consistent with the "Crispers on 436, complex directly across the road" description below. This retires the "Indigo Winter Park" candidate as a wrong guess — the circumstantial reasoning (SR 436/University Blvd corner, condo-unit building) pointed at the right corridor but the wrong specific complex. ## What was searched

Full Sail University's Winter Park campus sits at the corner of University Blvd and State Road 436 (Semoran Blvd). A Crispers restaurant at 1740 State Road 436, Winter Park, FL 32792 is documented (also a second, closed Crispers location at 480 N Orlando Ave — several miles away, on the wrong road, almost certainly not the one meant).

**Best candidate identified:** *Indigo Winter Park* (also listed commercially as "Indigo Condos"), 220 S Semoran Blvd, Winter Park, FL 32792 — described in current rental listings as sitting "on the corner of SR 436 and University Blvd... near Full Sail University (across the street)," and listed under "Indigo Condos Apartments" on at least one aggregator, consistent with a condo-unit building now operating under single-entity rental management — the same shape as an individually-owned unit (Suz's) inside a larger condo development. This is circumstantial identification only: no source found ties Suz Frank, or any Frank-family purchase, to this specific address, and no source confirms this was the complex's name or ownership structure circa 2008–2010 when the purchase would have happened (apartment complexes in this corridor are renamed and resold constantly; "Indigo" may postdate 2010 entirely).

## What was not found

No news report, court record, or local-media story matching the described human-trafficking arrest (a tenant who never paid rent, arrested a few months into a lease, running a scheme to recruit women about to be released from prison into sexual slavery) was located anywhere in this search. Searches tried: general Winter Park/Orlando human-trafficking arrest queries for 2010–2012, "bonded out of jail" trafficking schemes in Central Florida (which surfaced real but unrelated cases — Moncrief 2024, Martinez-Gonzalez/Wurster 2019–2021, both wrong decade), and searches combining Semoran Blvd/condo/landlord with trafficking terms.

This is a genuine negative result, not an exhausted one — a ~15-year-old local arrest is exactly the kind of story unlikely to surface in general web search without a name, an exact date, or a link to a specific news outlet's archive. A future pass with access to Orange County FL court records (case search by address or by "Winter Park" + trafficking statute) or the Orlando Sentinel's own archive search would have much better odds than open web search.
