# Robotussin's Last Dance

There really was no time quite like the summer after my Junior year of High School...this is partially because of the cultural era (the intersection of the fall out boy pop punk ascendance and the uber-sleaze of the Paris Hilton, paparazzi oopsie shot upskirt TMZ as the top pop culture story of the day. I wasn't then like how i am now...there was absolutely nothing intellectual or heady about 11th grade dan frank. 11th grade dan fank was most concerned with amassing enough abercrombie and fitch that he could basically buy his way into the senior superlative for best dressed - and i did.

I had had a lot of experience drinking and had been smoking weed sporadically for a few years...but this is pre-opiate crisis and it is also pre-any of my colleagues discovering cocaine yet. these were simple times. and in simple times you take simple drugs....and the most simple drug of all is the one you already have in your medicine cabinet...just waiting for you to take enough of it to shatter the space-time continuum.

Robotussin. Specifically, dextromethorphan. More specifically 20-40 red gel capsules of pure dextromethorphan. DXM. Robo-tripping.  This is how high schoolers got cooked in 2005.

In my life i have maybe done DXM 8-10 times. Though, these were all in a span of about 6 months before i graduated to much more professional and mature narcotics...but of those experiences, there is only one which stands out as the marquee experience into the adult drug world and it was one of the most fun and really dumb, stupid bad ideas i've ever been apart of.
