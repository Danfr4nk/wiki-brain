# Extreme Sports

From about age 11 until age 17, Dan's primary interest and aspirational focus was in several extreme sports - namely (street style) aggressive inline rollerblading in the summer and (slopestyle) terrain park skiing in the winter.

Dan most closely aligned with the freeskiing culture and was specifically a product of the TANNER HALL ERA. This was a moment where the skill threshold was blown out and in a number of years you go from the top tier hits were like Johnny Mosely doing a fucking dinner roll to someone like Candide Thovex who, all of the sudden, really fleshed out the idea that you COULD, in fact, cork 450 onto any  gap to flat down rail with incredible steez.

This was also a hinge point for 7 springs ski resort (the mountain where Dan's family owned a ski-in ski-out condo in the Villages - unit K2) and their park crew grew exponentially from season to season..as did the budget they allocated for new features. Growing up, Dan skied a mountain where there was one park on the north face and the grom park on the front face. the north face park had a 12ish foot halfpipe which always had chunks taken out of it and was placed with so little run-in that the entire excersize was almost without any purpose.  next to the pipe was a rope tow lift so hiking was unnecessary and then a slopestyle section with usually 3-4 features - two jumps where any inversion was precarious due to the small size of the jumps and two real park rails...usually a flat bar or a flat down.  the grom park was just 5 foot boxes and were mostly hit by 9 year olds with Volkl racing skis and a full face helmet as they run straight over the box, never attempting to spin frontside or backside.

However, by the season of 2006, 7 springs had fully developed into the premiere terrain park of the mid atlantic with a real SLOPE dedicated to several dozen features. i believe they bought an olympic sized pipe cutter and moved it. hired real park crews who knew how to build hits and were blessed with being flooded by money when the Nuttings took over the resort.

Interestingly, a few times a year, this absolute shredder would show up and make a MESS of the park for a day before deciding that the less developed terrain parks at roundtop and liberty were still preferable because of the vibrant collection of people in the scene and he would return there. That vibrant collection was so interesting and extraordinary that they would eventually take the sport by storm under the name 4bi9. That shredder who would come a few times a year - we would get to know him and his name was Tom Wallisch.

Come april, the less developed of the two sports for dan would come in season and because dan had and still has such an abysmal ollie that my Roces rollerblades were slapped on.
