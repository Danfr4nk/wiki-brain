## What this settles, corrects, or reopens

- **The AI dossiers' specific allegation is not supported.** No airfare dispute anywhere in this material. The "altered intern contracts" half of the dossier claim is corroborated — richly — but the mechanism (a clause barring recommendations without CEO approval, inserted into departing interns' contracts) is not what "airfare-billing discrepancies" describes. Treat the dossier line as a garbled compression of the real story, not a second source for it.

- **Exit date: February 2012, not the résumé's "Feb 2013."** Settled by the Feb 15 2012 "Error on final paycheck" email — Dan's last day was on or before that date.

- **Job title: "Special Projects Coordinator," not "Executive Assistant / Office Manager."** Contemporaneous signature and LinkedIn's own notice agree; `Resume.txt` (written years later) disagrees.

- **The real conflict has three acts, not one**: (1) a payroll dispute (missing W-2, wrong final check, disputed $104 MetroCard charge, escalated by Dan to the IRS and NYS Dept of Labor); (2) Dan's May 2012 unprompted whistleblower disclosure to a third party (Renae Holland) about Kevin altering intern contracts and using Dan's own account to pressure former employees; (3) a June 2012 retaliatory LinkedIn intellectual-property claim, backed by notarized false statements from a CL employee, that Dan successfully contested.

- **Not corroborated in this pass:** what specifically happened to Renae ("his attack against me"); the content of the "Astrology Will" Lisa was asked to return; the text of the notarized `Creative License 2.pdf` documents; whether LinkedIn ultimately ruled in Dan's favor (no resolution email located).
