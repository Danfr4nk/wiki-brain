### Biographical Data

- Full Name: Annie Celeste Ulmer

- Born: December 25, 1941

- Died: November 20, 2012 (age 24, or 71 if born 1941-2012-11-20)

- Location at death: Cleveland, Tennessee

### Education

- Cabrini High School - graduated 2006

- Xavier University of Louisiana - Doctorate in Pharmacy (2012)

### Professional History

- Worked at various pharmacies in New Orleans area during high school and college

- Employed at CVS Pharmacy in Cleveland, TN after graduation

### Family

- Parents: Evan and Toni Ulmer (devoted parents for 24 years)

- Grandparents: Ernest P. Ulmer, Jr. and the late Celeste Ulmer

- Also mentioned Michelle Rossi as a grandparent figure

- Survived by numerous aunts, uncles, cousins, and friends

### Death Details

- Incident: Double shooting at 1223 Crown St. NW, Cleveland, TN

- Victim: Annie Ulmer, 24, pronounced dead at Erlanger Hospital

- Survivor: Steven Lanosga, 28, treated and released

- Relationship: Ulmer and Lanosga were dating

- Cause/Manner: Pending autopsy results

### Personal Characteristics

- Animal lover

- Preferred donations to SPCA or Humane Society
