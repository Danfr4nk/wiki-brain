## 2. Directive on read notes

"May the task of doing this also be beneficial just for you to keep detailed notes for other analysis later? Just a thought"

Framed as a suggestion; adopted as method. The reasoning, stated so a future session does not quietly narrow it back:

**Reading is the expensive step and it is paid once.** Extracting a second kind of finding from an already-read window costs almost nothing; re-reading 97,768 messages to recover something noticed and not written down costs the whole pass again. A read that records only *events* therefore discards, at the moment of maximum information, everything that is not an event — which is precisely `CLAUDE.md` rule 1's failure mode ("anything dropped at extraction is a connection nobody can ever make"), applied to the most expensive extraction in the repository.

Concretely this adds a second output alongside the chronology: `wiki/timeline/annie-read-notes.md`, carrying the entity ledger, open leads, motif tracking with dated instances, a corrections queue against existing wiki claims, and quantitative markers worth counting later. The chronology answers "what happened"; the notes answer "what should someone look at next, and what did this window establish that isn't a dated event."
