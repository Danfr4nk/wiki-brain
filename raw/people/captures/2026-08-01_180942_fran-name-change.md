Fran (my great grandma) aka Frances T. Coldren was born Jesse Frances Coldren. Around 18 she changed it to Frances. The T in her name at death was for Thomas, an ode to Thomas Whyel, the coal and coke baron who was her second husband, that died in the 1970's and was added when she married her third husband Ira B. Coldren Jr, lawyer/west point grad and 33rd degree mason, several months after the death of her second husband.
