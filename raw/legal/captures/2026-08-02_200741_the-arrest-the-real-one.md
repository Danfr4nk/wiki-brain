It's sometime in early 2015. This is very much during the era when alexis had already been kicked out of my moms 337 house but before we made it to her 155 virginia ave house where she let me and lex live together for some time and that means that lex is secretly living in the house. this went on for about 4 months where i hid her, snuck her in and out, brought her food etc.

At about 2am, i get a text from Suz, who is, at this point in the THICK of her gambling addiction. She's at KJ's...betterknown as Anytime Billiards on Rt 51 in Uniontown. She asks me to bring her some lemons so that she can make lemon drops.

This particular night, I was without any substance. I had the teenyist tinyest little corner of a plastic bag saved to do a 10% bump at some point and i had enough weed to make mayyyybe a one hitter. Truly, and i mean this literally, i could not possibly have had any less drugs. I oblige, thinking i might get a line i had none that night.)

I had taken my mom's elderly and fully blind jack russel terrier Lucy with me to drop off the lemons. I had also taken, for some incredibly stupid reason, my little narcotics pack with me. On this night, I was driving Fran's car which hadn't been used since she had stopped driving. It was uninspected, the registration was out of date and the tires were so bald that anyone would have determined them to be unsafe.  In other words, I was asking for it.

As I'm going through downtown uniontown at 3am, returning from the lemon delivery, a state police vehicle behind me lights me up. I am basically right between  the front of my dad's store and the import store Romeo's across the street a few blocks away. Right in front of a hair dresser. I park and next thing i know, i'm out of the car.

The police would tell my mom that the reason they knew something was going on was because of how unnaturally polite and accomidating i was trying to be.  They were determined to search that car, and they thought i was holding something heavy.  I was never asked to complete field sobriety tests or take a roadside PBT. The car was not in my name so they attempted to go around me as a way to get permission for the search leading to one of the stupidest and funniest conversations of my life where suz, totally aware i am pulled over, just kept saying "well just let them search it " and i had to keep going "MmmmmmMMMMmmmmom that's not whatyou told me to do when you are asked to consent to a search"

Some time into this, they determine I am unable to drive the car home due to the condition of several things on it. Then they determine that, well - i'm parked somewhere that the car can't stay (it would have needed about 3 feet forward or backward) and so they just HAD to impound it, and when they impound a vehicle they have to perform an inventory search.

They tell me to take the dog and that i'm free to go. Knowing that they would soon find this shit i wasn't booking it out of there. But not 10 steps away, and with dog in arms, and i hear the asshole cop say "Hey how do you turn this on?" and when i turned around they put me in cuffs and arrest me for possession of a controlled substance.

On the way to the barracks for processing they actually stopped at the pool hall and gambling place my mom was in. Having a hard time believing that my mother was out partying on a random tuesday at 4am, I believe the purpose was to validate this part of my story. Addidionlly they were able to drop the dog off.

This is my first and only real arrest. I was charged for posession of the weed, paraphanlia for the one hitter and posession of a class B controlled substance for the RESIDUE COKE BAG. yes you heard that right. and to make matters worse, the DA at that time had a policy of not giving ARD to anyone with a cocaine or heroin misdemeanor charge. Residue or not.

Eventually, my fucking absolute LEGEND of a lawyer Jack Connor did get me into the ARD program and i completed that without incident. it cost several thousdan dollars and put me in fear for a few months but...i learned not to take stupid chances from my one and only arrest and that's a lesson that still feels potent to this day.
